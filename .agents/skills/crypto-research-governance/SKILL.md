# Crypto research governance

Use the repository's [current state](../../../docs/research/STATE.md) and
[evidence index](../../../docs/research/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) before a new hypothesis
or verdict. Historical program closure preserves the original claim and search
history; it does not prohibit a separately authorized, justified new program.
Reading a launch prompt or improving software does not itself launch research.

## Route the record before running

- Existing predlab families: `data/predlab/gates.json` and
  `data/predlab/trial_ledger.jsonl`.
- Existing rebuild/xsect families: `data/rebuild/gates.json` and its trial ledger;
  separately named LLM families retain their own `data/llm_*_xs/` registries.
- Corrections: the new committed gate/charter names its original families,
  preserved inputs and exact ledger/output destinations; append the explanation
  to `docs/audit/corrections.jsonl` only as its declared policy requires.
- Quote measurements and engineering checks are not financial trial rows.
  Preserve their declared separate manifests/ledgers. The completed dated-carry
  example uses `data/carry-feasibility/2026-09-10/measurement_ledger.jsonl`.
- New programs: use the [run-admission contract](../../../docs/research/README.md)
  with a new committed registration. Link parent hypotheses, cumulative budgets
  and exposed samples; do not reset them by renaming a family. A lifecycle
  receipt does not replace the charter, substantive gates or financial ledger
  obligations declared for that experiment.

Freeze inputs, chronology, full grid, baselines, costs, effect/exposure/risk
thresholds, uncertainty/multiplicity treatment, resource bounds, forensics and
selection/stop rules before outcomes. A missing registration means prepare the
registration, not run results. Development can adapt under a new declared
question; inspected development does not become fresh confirmation. Commit the
selection before opening genuinely untouched confirmation and follow its
predeclared sequential-look policy.

## Make the claim match the measurement

Use explicit signed quantities/cashflows or valid simple-return accounting,
according to the [engine contract](../../../docs/research/ENGINE_INDEX.md).
Log returns may construct a signal/forecast or a disclosed convention-swap
diagnostic; they are never arithmetic trading PnL. Admit fees, funding,
settlement, timing, executable prices, cash reserves and instrument identities
before computing net wealth. Unknowns remain unknown rather than zero or a
convenient substitute.

Check source/data identity and every registered cell, including unavailable and
failed attempts. Verify both positive and negative results with the charter's
independent cashflow checks, null/power/leakage probes and convention diagnostic.
Do not mirror implementation output as the only correctness test. Keep
assumed/proxy economics conditional; synthetic verification proves accounting
behavior, not profitability.

Distinguish a specific adoption failure from absent mechanism, excessive costs,
unwanted exposure, insufficient power, unavailable data or defective measurement.
Follow-up requires an evidenced question and a new registration, not favorable
parameter selection after a near-pass. A strategy is validated only when its
entire frozen confirmation, feasibility and forensic process supports the claim.
Update findings and durable state with source/data/gate/result references.
