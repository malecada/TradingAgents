## Zero-exposure control adapter

The adapter accepts only wallet-cash and wallet-ETH25. It requires the exact
ETH/USDC input universe; the unchanged underlying engine validates the complete
calendar and positive exact held marks. The added WST price is internal positive
padding for a zero holding, not a sourced quote or valuation of missing exposure.

`check_progress` checks initial/current/terminal quantities, every state and
event, and the literal ledger's WST balance/deltas. The final output removes WST
padding from initial/final balances, state prices, event quantities, partial
current prices and literal balance rows. The zero wrapper-loss tail is removed;
other stresses still apply to the actual held ETH/USDC exposure. Explicit flags
identify verified zero exposure and non-observed padding.

Partial snapshots use the new literal receipt serializer, not the frozen
wrapper's high-level-event replay. A funded ETH purchase followed by an invented
mark failure retains exact balances and the authoritative conversion even when
the valued event is absent. Sentinel values cannot authorize F2 or any nonzero
WST position. This remains a restricted reuse adapter, not a general missing-
asset imputation mechanism.

The three adapter tests pass, including varying the unused sentinel to very
large positive values, preservation of actual ETH/USDC marks, rejection of F2
and missing held prices, and post-mutation partial preservation. The preceding
independent collector review additionally records six policy/scenario pairs
invariant to sentinel1 versus alternating10^80/10^-80.

## Financial routing and decision separation

F1 contains one fixed numerical lending book per registered cost scenario,
explicitly using the cap-assumed diagnostic role. A separate strict entry cell
keeps a nonzero unproved cap unavailable. No missing debt/treasury value is
replaced with zero or turned into a proof flag. The endpoint opportunity ceiling
is separate, can survive irrelevant daily operational gaps, and cannot rescue
the numerical book or primary decision.

Wallet controls can run when the candidate's protocol fields are unavailable
but their complete price panel remains qualified. A missing terminal held price
blocks both the relevant ceiling and wallet books. Complete candidate source
and boundary witness requirements remain explicit. Boundary witnesses qualify
the declared conditional source model, not deployed historical behavior,
executable prices, personal account access or unchanged counterfactual paths.

The two earlier fixed cohorts retain unavailable book/auxiliary cells without
backfilling or running them. Only the latest fixed cohort supplies D comparisons.
F1 has73 financial cells and67 outputs:33 book/auxiliary cells across the three
cohorts,36 fixed comparison cells and four boundary/decision/implementation/
confirmation cells. F3 has79 cells and73 outputs, including its matched-entry
inventory control and39 fixed comparison cells. These are the reviewed routing
denominators, not independent hypothesis counts or evidence of available data.

Each book and auxiliary calculation has a durable attempted/reason/time intent
before its economic calculation. Expected `FinancialUnavailable` exclusions
remain cell-local; an unexpected arithmetic/measurement defect records the
partial book and suppresses later economic attempts. All remaining unavailable
outputs and cells are retained. A storage/callback failure still requires outer
lifecycle partial closure; this helper cannot guarantee persisted outputs after
arbitrary storage failure.

P uses each book's exact signed full-capital result. D subtracts exact rational
P for the new books; only frozen legacy records without exact fractions use
their retained decimal strings. All fixed comparators remain visible, including
unavailable B1. Legacy CEX comparisons explicitly retain their differing
oracle/bar/route basis. Risk eligibility is separately reported; no favorable
comparator is selected after observing its difference.

The primary, implementation and confirmation outputs remain unavailable.
`known_comparator_numerical_pass_only` is a descriptive conjunction over the
known risk-eligible comparison subset, whose count is also reported. It is not
proof that every mandatory benchmark is available or that strict cap/lock/
execution/confirmation requirements passed. Positive P, favorable D, favorable
risk or an opportunity ceiling cannot promote the strategy.
