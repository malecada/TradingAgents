## Q3: native staking, wrapper markets and redemption

**Economic question.** Can a low-turnover, unhedged staking/cash allocation add
net cash value over the identical initial ETH/cash exposure, while the full
account meets the accepted annual return and risk conditions? Validator rewards
are one channel; ETH beta, wrapper discount, exit costs and delayed redemption
are competing explanations. The old WBETH hedge already supplied a negative
full-capital relevance finding. Its two claims and windows remain inherited.

**Cheapest source question.** Establish the actual native wrapper conversion
interface and queue state on Ethereum, and the separate bridged-token interface
on Base, before asking for a full financial panel. The original Ethereum archive
failure is evidence about the tested endpoint/height, not a failed staking
mechanism or permission to repeat the same probe until it succeeds. Any additional
historical route must be named and justified before acquisition, with a bounded
request inventory. No present conversion ratio is backfilled into old dates.

Proposed source fields are canonical chain/header/code identities; native wrapper
stEthPerToken/tokensPerStEth and a fixed-unit conversion consistency check;
queue last request/finalized IDs, unfinalized request count, unfinalized stETH,
locked ETH and bunker state. No wallet owner, user balance or request belonging
to the user is queried. These are interface/capacity context, not APR, profit or
a historical wait distribution. The exact fields/calldata/epochs and request
denominator must be committed in the actual Q3 gate.

For a financial book, choose a single exit policy before outcomes: market sale
requires a two-way executable wrapper quote and gas; native queued exit requires
historical request/finalization/claim accounting and the policy's time of request.
Selecting the better of market sale and redemption after observing the outcome
is another strategy. Aggregate queue counts cannot supply the counterfactual
request's exact waiting time. A queue request no longer earns holder rewards in
the same way, while remaining exposed to losses. Missing exit fields therefore
block implementability even if wrapper conversion is observable.

**Falsification and informative negatives.** A frozen book can identify whether
incremental rewards are consumed by costs, discounts or losses. It can also show
that the staking sleeve adds value but the full account misses10%. These are
different results. Source failure or insufficient future evidence is not an
economic rejection. Raising capital earns a scenario only when fixed-cost
accounting, rather than price risk or unknown exitability, is the blocker.

## Q4: mature small-token adoption, without survivor selection

**Economic question.** Persistent use of a token-linked product may create token
demand before price reflects it. The required link is between product use and
tokenholder value: fees paid in the token, credibly enforced distributions or
another explicit demand mechanism. Transaction/address growth alone is weak
evidence; wash activity, incentives, common ETH beta, insider distribution and
new issuance can explain it. A governance token need not capture product growth.

The draft $10m–$250m circulating cap and age90days are still proposals, not
admitted filters. A current market-cap list cannot reconstruct the eligible
universe. Fully diluted capitalization is a different definition and would need
an explicit choice before outcomes. No cap threshold or horizon is tuned after
seeing returns.

**What can be reused.** The retained NLST pipeline enumerated Ethereum v2 pair
creation logs and screened first-day liquidity/swaps, then acquired roughly16days
of pool events. NLST3 used wallet/deployer histories and feature composites;
SMW already used v2/v3 wallet flows and buyer-breadth acceleration. Their code is
useful for identifying raw fields and failure modes, but their old fetchers and
experiment mains are not execution routes for this phase. The code's saved paths
are candidates, not proof the original raw bytes are present and complete.

Read-only source-code orientation used:
`scripts/predlab_nlst_dex_fetch.py`, `predlab_nlst3_features.py`,
`predlab_nlst4_features.py`, `predlab_smw_fetch.py`, `predlab_smw_features.py`.
No raw arrays, new token ranking or financial recomputation was performed.
Old feature builders include approximate/interpolated clocks and contemporaneous
contract classifications that need their later correction history; reuse is
not automatic point-in-time admission.

**Cheapest informative source inspection.** Freeze an exact metadata inventory
of retained universe/manifests/schema contracts. Inspect only whether it can
support these requirements, preserving every absent item:

1. Chain/contract/pool/version identity and migrations, with a universe that
   retains subsequently failed and delisted tokens.
2. Birth and eligibility dates available at the decision, with sufficient
   pre-decision history for the chosen mature-token definition.
3. Historical circulating supply and unlock/dilution schedules, with revision
   and publication clocks, or an explicitly different universe definition.
4. A measurable link from product adoption to tokenholder demand/value and
   causal observation times. Wallet copying remains the already-searched family.
5. Two-way liquidity, token transfer/sell restrictions, fees, gas and liquidation
   for the entire holding period. A buyable token need not be sellable later.
6. Failed cases and denominators before price joining. Missing prices cannot
   remove losing positions; neither can they be called actual zero recovery
   without a declared valuation contract and evidence.

A16day new-pool panel does not itself answer a90day-age/365day allocation
question. This is a coverage mismatch to investigate, not proof that mature
small-token adoption cannot work. A longer horizon or another chain does not
erase the44related NLST/SMW/T7 records, correction exposure or unknown wider
multiplicity. An F4 descendant requires its explicit inherited allowance and
exact causal universe; an unused reserved slot is insufficient by itself.
