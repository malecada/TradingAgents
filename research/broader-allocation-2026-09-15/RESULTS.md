## Economic interpretation

- **H2:** Band rebalancing saved trading but added only $9.69 versus held 25% BTC and trailed monthly rebalancing. Lower crypto exposure explains much of the advantage over fully invested holdings in this falling cohort.
- **H3:** The slow trend filter reduced exposure and observed drawdown, but delayed exits/entries still left a loss. Its improvement over simple BTC allocations falls short of the frozen incremental hurdle. It is not selected as a winner merely because its loss is smaller.
- **H4:** Phased purchases reduced initial exposure and loss versus held 25% BTC. They still underperformed cash and monthly 25% rebalancing; the observed result does not establish a timing premium.
- **H5:** Volatility scaling added trades and underperformed both 25% BTC controls. The registered estimator and timing did not add useful economic value in this cohort; no parameter rescue is admitted.
