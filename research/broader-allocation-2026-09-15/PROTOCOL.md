# Admission, cash accounting and confirmation

## Work completed before experiments

The design/history documents and inventory are preparation, not a `gates.json`
registration. No executable registration with placeholder preferences, empty
history or guessed hashes is created. Exact dates, access, cash terms, economic
preferences, source files and inference choices must be resolved first.

The proposed source-admission queue is finite:

1. Resolve the investment contract and accessible cash/spot/FX implementation.
2. Reconcile exact H2–H5 ancestor/config/sample IDs and required allowance amendments.
3. Audit named existing spot sources and historical filters against the needed
   daily signals and action prices; classify proxy-only/unavailable fields.
4. Check custody, transfer/prefund timing, lots and cash costs at $10,000.
   If capital is the blocker, derive and freeze one justified C_plus using
   DESIGN before outcomes; report the minimum required amount and uncertainty.
5. Verify a pooled signed-quantity cash engine with independent synthetic books.
6. Freeze the development panel, precision/power design and confirmation route.

Metadata-only engineering checks are not financial trials. New quote/source
measurements still require their own committed charter and separate measurement
ledger. Every financial evaluation is registered before outcomes. A question
using empirical variability for power is a registered development measurement,
not an uncounted look. No market data is fetched during this design.

- Complete grid: four recipes, ten benchmarks, $10,000 baseline and optionally

  per window with C_plus, even if scenarios coincide or a cell is unavailable.
  The capital constraint, amount derivation and eligibility decision are frozen

- Source/dependency/runtime hashes, full charter hash, input hashes, cell IDs,
  output manifest, financial ledger contract, cost/convention/stress diagnostics,
  decision, multiplicity/power method, resource limits and stop rules.
- The concrete independent-review assignment and evidence the design was
  committed before the relevant outcome window. Local Git timestamps alone
  are not externally witnessed preregistration. Verify actual remote commit
  recovery before prospective confirmation under the authorized backup route.

Run admission-only `python -m tradingagents.research check` with the real
committed registration, experiment ID and full source SHA; this is not a run.

allowance. Use the separate `verify` receipt checker plus substantive review.

field: retain a full ancestry crosswalk in the charter/history and use the direct

## Complete cash book

For each wallet/asset, retain quantities and transaction cashflows. In the
reporting currency, liquidation wealth is cash across all committed locations
plus sellable quantities at executable liquidation bid, minus exit, FX,
redemption, transfer and remaining liabilities. It is not derivative notional
and not the sum of independent sleeve return indices.

Record cash before/after each action, fee currency/base, rounded quantity,
execution price/depth, idle cash, interest actually accrued, external capital
flows (proposed zero after inception), receivables in transit and dust. Return
is `(ending liquidation wealth - initial C) / C` with no outside contributions.
Initial acquisition and final liquidation costs apply to passive holdings too;
cash does not pay fictional exchange fees when it stays outside the venue.
No double counting of spread in both executable price and a cost surcharge.

Use the existing [linear/event accounting contracts](../../docs/research/ENGINE_INDEX.md)
only after testing their spot inventory/cash and timing boundaries. Prefer a
small spot/cash adapter to a new research platform. No legacy experiment main
may be executed for convenience. Genuine spot has no perpetual funding; fee
assets and FX still matter. A later derivative/claim extension needs its own
funding, settlement, collateral and lifetime engine admission.

Fees are proposed conservatively at 10 basis points per traded side without
discounts, plus observed spread and depth/slippage, until exact applicable terms
are admitted and frozen. This is a planning assumption, not a verified user fee.
Model any minimum/fixed banking/withdrawal/network fee and fee-asset purchase.
If prices are only bars, retain conditional scenarios with a declared execution
proxy; do not label them executable fills or use them for an implementation pass.
Doubled-cost sensitivity doubles fees, spread and impact consistently; the
frictionless diagnostic excludes these trading frictions but retains capital,
risk and all non-trading cash economics. Neither can replace the primary book.

Signals use completed information; no same-bar fill. Proposed monthly decision
is first calendar day at 00:05 UTC, using daily information through the prior
00:00 UTC close. Orders are valued at the first admitted quote at/after the
action time within the frozen timeout. That quote must have realistic available
size. Cash transfers must precede buys; a later source protocol must freeze a
feasible prefunding delay/reserve, never assume instant free off-venue funding.
No financial run until those concrete timing/cost dependencies are solved.

If a scheduled trade is below lot/minimum rules, retain cash/holdings according
to the registered skip rule and record the unexecuted instruction. Missing held
price, unknown custody recovery, unknown cash rate or fee applicability is
unknown, never zero. Keep the whole unavailable cell with first missing cause
and known partial flows; do not drop dates, forward-fill terminal proceeds or
refetch a substitute sample. Warmup insufficiency is unavailable, not a changed
lookback. Missing action data follows the frozen fail/skip policy and cannot
be chosen after profitability is observed.

Independent review reconstructs literal cash examples and selected actual books
from input evidence, checks sources/units/clocks and reconciles every attempted
cell. Synthetic cases include round-trip costs, base-paid fees, lot dust, idle
cash interest, delayed transfers, missing held marks, adverse gaps and final FX.
The convention diagnostic substitutes log returns on the frozen exposure path
only to reveal classification differences; signed quantities/simple returns
govern PnL throughout. Repeat only appropriate offline tests after code changes;
synthetic success is not evidence of economic edge.

## Inference and untouched confirmation

Development uses only explicitly exposed samples under a registered grid. At
most three windows must be fixed for chronology/coverage before opening arrays;
rolling start-date searches, horizon/sign changes and fee rescues are prohibited.
Retain null comparisons that distinguish timing from simple exposure/cash:
static B5/B9 and a predeclared dependence-preserving timing placebo, charging
turnover/costs afresh. Do not use a hindsight exposure match as an investable rule.
Every placebo, stress and sensitivity is counted as a diagnostic, never a new
chance to choose the headline metric.

Select a recipe under DESIGN using development only, then commit the exact
policy, benchmarks, accepted contract and full selection history before opening
untouched evidence. Prefer prospective evidence after the existing options
outcome inspection is finished, so that concurrent selection cannot exploit
the same crypto path. No prospective start date is invented here; freeze the
first eligible date after all admission, selection and review steps.

Use one terminal statistical look. Intermediate access is limited to source
integrity; any economic look is recorded as exposure and prevents a fresh claim
unless the original sequential procedure explicitly allowed it. No optional
stopping for profit, early promotion, automatic restart or extension after a
near miss. A defective run preserves every artifact and consumes its budget.

Proposed inference family reserves four original recipes x two possible capital
scenarios x six contrasts (absolute P plus five primary benchmark differences):
**48 tests maximum**. Use one-sided Bonferroni alpha 0.05/48 or an independently
reviewed simultaneous method frozen before data; these are not independent
hypotheses. Retain that conservative allocation if only the baseline is used
or only one recipe/capital combination reaches confirmation. Historical
unknown search multiplicity prohibits inferential validation on old data;
future fixed selection and genuine untouched evidence are essential.

Before activation, fix the expected-annual-cash estimator and uncertainty method
with dependence-preserving simulation on registered development data and
synthetic nulls. Report statistical rejection power at the proposed 2%-of-C
annual incremental effect ($200 at baseline), with family error controlled at 5%. Do not call this
the probability of passing the entire adoption rule: when a true mean equals
an observed-effect threshold, that threshold alone passes about half the time
under a symmetric sampling model, regardless of how precisely it is measured.

The proposed **confirmation admission** condition is at least 80% simulated
joint adoption-gate power at a separately fixed planning alternative above the
effect floors: true expected annual P = 10% of C and minimum expected D across
primary comparators = 4% of C. These are hypothetical design alternatives,
not return forecasts or additional investment preferences. Freeze a coherent
joint candidate/benchmark/cash path model, dependence and risk assumptions,
then simulate the entire conjunction: absolute profit, all benchmark contrasts,
family confidence bounds, risk and doubled-cost gates. Report its sensitivity
to the model and effect assumptions, plus rejection power at the 2% boundary.
Passing the statistical component alone does not admit confirmation. If no
defensible model can establish this condition within the finite horizon,
confirmation remains inadequately supported; never assume missing power is 80%.

A 365-day path is not 365 independent
annual trials; overlapping annual windows are not independent replication.
Block length, simulation count, seed, stationarity assumptions and minimum
effective sample size must be fixed in the experiment charter. Bootstrap cannot
create unseen regimes or validate a rare-loss probability.

Illustration, not an empirical estimate: a 2%-of-capital annual difference with
10% annual paired SD needs approximately 155 independent years for 80% power
at even unadjusted one-sided 5%, using `((1.645+0.842)*0.10/0.02)^2`.
Multiplicity would increase that requirement. Therefore the proposed 365/730-day
bound may support accounting/feasibility and useful descriptive findings yet
be insufficient for subtle expected-return superiority. If the registered power
test fails within the finite bound, record **inadequate evidence**, not no edge;
do not weaken the effect/metric/confidence rule to manufacture validation.

## Decisions retained after every question

Classify each claim separately: economic failure (fully measured costs/returns),
risk/implementation failure, unavailable data/access, inadequate evidence,
measurement defect, or qualified progression. Retain small positive absolute
profit even if benchmark value fails, and benchmark improvement even if absolute
profit is negative. Record what the result weakens, what it cannot establish,
the full trial denominator, budget used and next eligible ranked question.

At the finite boundary, independent coverage review determines whether any
unanswered admitted question remains. A paused preference decision, missing
data or insufficient time is not economic exhaustion. No validated strategy
is a legitimate eventual conclusion. Useful negative findings stay available
to prevent repeated search, while the options study continues under its own
frozen contract.

## Public primary-source notes, reviewed September 15

- [Binance fee explanation](https://www.binance.com/en-ZA/support/faq/detail/e85d6e703b874674840122196b89780a)
  explains per-trade fees and gives a 0.1% regular-user example. It does not
  establish this account's pair/entity/discount/tax schedule.
- [Official spot filters](https://github.com/binance/binance-spot-api-docs/blob/master/filters.md)
  document quantity steps, price ticks and notional filters; actual symbols and
  historical/current applicability must be admitted separately.
- [Official public archives](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data)
  document kline/trade archives, checksum companions and replacements. These
  are a possible low-cost source, not historical executable depth or local
  receipt witnesses. New archive versions retain prior hashes and exposure.
- [Liu and Tsyvinski, Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877)
  provides historical momentum/attention motivation in the indexed author
  abstract. Direct retrieval returned 403 in this review; no full-paper
  replication or modern small-account net-profit claim is made. The hypothesis
  substantially overlaps the repository's earlier factor/attention work.

Only public documents were consulted; no economic data acquisition, backtest,
paid source, account operation or external communication occurred.
