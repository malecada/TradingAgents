# Remaining raw-to-population admission requirements

This is an interface and admission checkpoint, not an executable release. The
existing fit controller consumes immutable prepared populations. It does not yet
decode the entire raw study history or freeze those populations itself. A passed
synthetic controller test therefore does not establish full empirical integration.

Every proposed preparation registration must fix its exact source identities,
asset/date/field denominator, graph configuration, complete-week boundaries,
coverage and exclusion rules, allowed reuse, outputs and resource limits before
decoding. All calendar cells remain represented even when source data is missing.
BTC projection requires source-resolved prevouts and the declared cross-transaction
spend/chain checks; partitioning must not silently discard those checks at a year
or week boundary. Graphs spanning source-year boundaries need all constituent
days. Neither source-list completeness nor a successful HTTP request admits a
canonical-chain graph by itself.

Each raw-to-graph transform needs one exclusive durable owner keyed by source
and configuration. A completed graph is reused through its immutable manifest
and verified members. Existing completed pilot graphs and original historical
results must not be recomputed under a convenient new identity. Partial scratch
state, failures and source exclusions remain preserved. A checkpoint is not
permission to reopen a terminal claim; any permitted successor must bind its
parent, exact completed prefix, new identity and cumulative allowance.

After graph admission and before any dictionary, learned comparator or model
fit, produce and freeze the common examples, labels, training membership,
evaluation mask and scaler provenance. Freeze missing-week/price exclusions from
data availability, never from which arm happens to finish. Use the exact
Coin Metrics amendment for the captured prices, retain original Yahoo failures,
and do not blend providers or change the reconstructed UTC calendar. Dictionary
sampling/clustering and fitted preprocessing use only the declared training
population. All comparators and variants share the appropriate frozen population;
test outcomes cannot amend it.

The current allocation has 24 consumed of 51 cumulative claims, with all 1,420
unique financial fits still pending. Body capture, raw-to-graph assembly,
population preparation and fitting cannot silently become extra uncounted
experiments. A release must either explicitly include preparation phases within
an already reserved finite claim or obtain a reviewed prospective cumulative
allocation amendment. A claim combining several phases must retain every phase
and downstream unavailable cell if an earlier phase fails. The unchanged family
contract cannot be replaced while an active claim depends on it.

Capacity is an independent admission condition. The reviewed two-week projection
under the current uncompressed artifact format is approximately 407.184 GB for the initial ETH graph/MCM arrays alone with the
1.5 planning margin. Full-node MCM and neural capacity have not been measured to
completion. Current disk headroom and frozen neighborhood/memory limits do not
admit the full workload. The largest-week pilot ended under an ancestor memory-pressure kill; all
109 completed/unavailable dispositions remain preserved. A successor would need
a new reviewed identity, capacity contract and cumulative allocation. No acquisition, fit, reduced graph, larger
cap, new hardware rental or provider contact is authorized by this document.
