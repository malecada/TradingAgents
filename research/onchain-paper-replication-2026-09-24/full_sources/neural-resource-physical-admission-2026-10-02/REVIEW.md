## Confirmed material boundaries

1. `neural_resource.py:268` finalizes only allocated producer bytes plus `run.directory/outputs`. Claim/terminal metadata, the lifecycle root and launch/guard/observer tree are explicitly excluded. The draft64MiB value therefore cannot be described as a complete-workflow allowance. Nine4MiB checkpoint ceilings total37,748,736 bytes; adding the existing1MiB admission margin gives38,797,312. The checkpoint write cap and eventual allocated-byte checks do not bound every intermediate metadata write or resident-memory peak.
2. `job.py:45` constructs the optional single existing-root watcher during admission; `job.py:164` subsequently creates the exclusive launch base. `resources.py:200` constructs its watcher before creating the guard receipt directory. `lifecycle.py:78` and `neural_resource.py:200` independently create the claim and producer roots. Precreating these roots defeats existing exclusive-creation semantics. An existing checkout ancestor could technically cover all three trees, but would include historical/unrelated content and need its own traversal/baseline policy. The report correctly limits its claim to the absence of exact fresh-attempt coverage under the current schema, rather than claiming any ancestor accounting is impossible.
3. `resources.py:281` routes stdout and stderr to uncapped `append:child.log`. Neither the wall limit nor the saved sizes supply a write-rate or file-extent bound. A complete new route must bound actual output writes and retain overflow/error disposition; periodic observation alone leaves unbounded inter-sample growth.
4. `resources.py:369` samples storage before final `live.json`/`final.json` publication. `job.py:331` onward can subsequently retain observer/postmortem/death/unsealed evidence and lifecycle failure. The report correctly includes this terminal tail. Merely adding watched roots or a log cap would leave the physical denominator incomplete. Reserved, bounded final writes and final reconciliation of the complete selected root set are necessary, including failure paths.
5. `workflow_storage.py:48` refuses regular files whose link count differs from one, whereas `lifecycle.py:31` publishes immutable JSON through a temporary inode, a second hard link and subsequent unlink. A concurrent scan can therefore reject a valid publication interval. The proposed additive route must coordinate or otherwise precisely recognize its own publication transaction without broadly permitting arbitrary hardlinks. This is a source-derived race; no synthetic reproduction was run during this review.

The proposed correction boundary is coherent: job ownership/root creation and exact policy binding; guard log and receipt writes; an additive accounting/I/O helper; and lifecycle claim/terminal publication or a narrow caller interface that can actually reserve and enforce those writes. Source/claim identity must be bound at the appropriate creation phase, since the lifecycle claim does not exist at launch-base birth. Final values and detailed API choices are intentionally unresolved. A root list alone is insufficient. Existing historical profiles and artifacts should remain unchanged; no empirical limit can be inferred from this handoff.
