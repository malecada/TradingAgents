## Implementation

`verify_prefix` accepts only an exact genuine ResearchRun. It checks current admission/claim/source, actual registered parent claim and FAILED terminal paths, family and parent joins, a fresh successor identity/allowance, accepted hash-bound review, separate score/offload and numeric/origin recovery inventories and their original evidence references. A parent accounting output must be an actual hash-named output of the failed run. Completed and retained partial costs cannot be refunded.

Every imported batch authenticates pending/header/typed-score/completion bytes, ordered origins and corresponding original scores, numeric summary, operation/checkpoint counters and durable marker or explicit legacy counter provenance. Counters remain cumulative within an execution epoch; a reviewed fresh memo boundary starts a new local epoch and retains the parent's total spent costs. Copied origin/score/summary bodies are unchanged.

Only immutable registered role/hash references, bounded descriptor bytes and scalar metadata are retained in CompletedPrefix. Every purpose/read/import rehydrates registered bytes through current source-checked reads. At most the current and one previous origin batch are decoded during verification; prior-origin rereads are counted. The driver reconstructs imported purpose keys from each authentic parent descriptor, while new scores retain the successor's actual operational descriptor. The stable scientific numerical contract is separate from claim/source/workload provenance.

The explicit continuation metadata reader bounds regular, single-link, same-device files before allocation at 32 MiB, with descriptor-relative no-follow access and source/claim checks at both transaction boundaries. General lifecycle input limits remain unchanged. Six batch bodies use the existing grouped reader with 1 MiB each and 4 MiB total. `CAPACITY02.json` derives the actual largest graph's 17,700 batches from preserved pilot35 typed_payload SHA `5902c014c2569aad172df3ad6ec0a202c20145db33647a5dfc2204e623514f23`; the worst-case bounded reference roster occupies 22,974,601 bytes and fits the metadata limit. This is capacity arithmetic, not measured full-prefix throughput. Genuine per-batch source admission may still be costly.

Future markers are written only after score, origin and summary closure, before retirement. Restored legacy accounting retains its actual body under `.legacy-prefix-counter.json`; no historical `.prefix.json` is invented. Fully completed graphs, including short final batches, are supported with zero fresh computation. Imported operational-policy schemas are validated independently; successor policy equality applies to new summaries. The accepted scientific contract/review must authorize that operational difference.

Legacy absent-policy driver behavior stays selected by default. Numeric storage's pre-existing `resume_supported=false` continues to describe absence of arbitrary numeric-state/cache checkpoint restoration; completed-prefix import is the explicit separate versioned route.
