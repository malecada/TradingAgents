# Compact scientific sample proof

The maintained `compact_sample_proof.py` accepts only the actual current-owner
Published sample object and its admitted Training/Draws ancestry. It verifies
existing sampled evidence without invoking the sampler or selecting a new center.
Admission creates no output namespace or matching pair work. Complete calendar,
prices/labels and empirical admission remain separate from this sample proof.

The semantic audit requires exact configuration/order SampleManifest identity,
seed and ordered training population; unique recorded centers; exact local
parent/center/dimensions; complete induced neighborhoods using the registered
ArrayNeighborhoodIndex policy; selected-index hashes; retained numeric-byte
prefixes; and ordered typed local identities. Actual training parent hashes are
rechecked around the audit and after its final live callback.

The overlap-weight audit retains two N-sized float64 vectors within the admitted
16*N direct-weight allowance. It independently computes each recorded center's
probability, normalizes the cumulative probabilities as the pinned NumPy choice
does, and checks that the saved PCG64 uniform lies inside that recorded center's
CDF interval. It zeroes the chosen center and halves all selected neighborhood
weights. No `choice` or `sample` call occurs in this audit. This reconstructs a
saved selection for verification; it does not create another stochastic sample
or replay a terminal research attempt. The selected index policy and outer guard
remain required for extraction buffers, metadata, parent graphs and process RSS.

The resulting dictionary workload scope contains the exact ordered typed sample
identities, sample identity, training-bound dictionary configuration, matching
configuration, scalar backend, seed and compact workflow identity. The separate
proof marks sample provenance admitted while keeping complete-calendar and
representation admission false. Inner leases assume frozen resident inputs;
full checks repeat the audit and final callback-free draw/artifact/parent joins.
