# Independent review — weighted-choice feasibility probe

September 29, 2026. Reviewed `probe.py`, compact result/evidence metadata, `RESULT.md`, the saved final guard receipt and the current sampler's choice/update call sites. No probe, test, empirical job or network request was run; no `.bin` contents were read. Only this review was written.

**No material overstatement of the stated finite synthetic parity was found.** The probe compares two separately seeded PCG64 generators. At each draw, the eager side invokes the actual NumPy `Generator.choice` with its normalized float64 weights; the mapped side performs sum/divide, cumulative sum, final-CDF normalization, a scalar uniform draw and right-side search. Assertions compare selected index, selected probability bytes and complete PCG64 state after every draw. A final array-equality assertion checks the accumulated weight updates. These are substantive comparisons against the library call, rather than two calls to the proposed implementation.

The reported 15 unique cases are exactly three candidate counts × five seeds. Draw counts are 256 for each 257-candidate case and 512 otherwise: `5 × (256 + 512 + 512) = 6,400`. Reported mapped payload bytes equal three float64 arrays, `24 × candidates`. All three hashes in `evidence.json` match the inspected files. The final guard records child 0, verified cleanup, zero memory-limit events, 7.991773383 seconds and 49,618,944 peak sampled bytes, agreeing with RESULT.md.

The executable refuses any `np.__version__` other than 2.3.0, and the saved result records that version. The cited URL is the official NumPy repository's `v2.3.0` `_generator.pyx` source path. Its remote bytes were not independently fetched or audited in this review, nor was installed-wheel provenance independently reconstructed. The finite behavior claim is supported by the actual library-call comparisons and saved execution evidence; this is not a proof over all NumPy inputs or builds.

The result correctly limits itself to feasibility. It checks selected probability bytes, not every entry of every probability/CDF array. The synthetic updates start at unit weights and use zeroing and powers-of-two halving over deterministic overlapping index neighborhoods; actual graph membership, center ordering, neighborhood construction and complete sample-manifest parity are absent. One wording detail: `probe.py:39` says “positive finite weights,” but selected weights become zero; the exercised condition is nonnegative finite weights with positive remaining total. Invalid, empty, exhausted, extreme-underflow and arbitrary-weight cases are not covered.

The peak measurement includes the eager oracle and small fixtures; it establishes neither production memory savings nor full-fold scalability. Durable production checkpoints, interruption recovery, scratch admission, failure preservation and integration are explicitly pending. Current production sampling remains unchanged. This report supplies no empirical or production release.
