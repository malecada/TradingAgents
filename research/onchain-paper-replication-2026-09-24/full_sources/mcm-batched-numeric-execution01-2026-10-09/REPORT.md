## Concrete recording and guards

At the last cell, actual memo.end_batch must succeed before writing any origin bytes or summary for that batch. Origin bytes are buffered up to4096*9=36,864B, written/read back/fsynced once per completed batch (short writes loop), then an exclusive bounded summary is written/read back/fsynced and its directory fsynced. The summary binds range, exact computed/reused counts, origin-range hash, ordered receipt hash, actual memo counters, elapsed memo-call seconds and old_per_occurrence_execution_credit=false. Cumulative summary and origin byte bounds are checked before writes. Partial-write failure poisons, retains partial evidence and grants no summary credit.

The binding includes explicit limits/ranges, origin inode/signature/hash, batch-directory identity/signature, ordered summary-body hash and summary file-signature inventory, counts/counters and timing. Counters are measured at finish before close clears the cache (and increments eviction counters); elapsed_seconds measures only memo calls, excluding separate begin/end guards and journal IO. It is not a performance benchmark.

The verifier rejects unexpected summary names, missing summaries, wrong extents, file type/mode/link or current signatures, invalid mode/origin ordering, count/hash/inventory/range disagreements and changed bodies. It streams one batch at a time and rechecks summary signatures. It verifies that computed origins equal their own ordinal and reused origins precede their occurrence.
