# Held score reader implemented source candidate

`held_score_reader.py` implements the actual local context/reader, not merely an interface. It derives and verifies its source from genuine imported Target/current Owner/active Stage/original held token and the original MCMScoreStream. It opens the unchanged reader02 with a derived batch terminal and checks original authority/content before and after reads and final closure. Ordinary rebinding/deletion is rejected through slots and attribute guards. Cleanup failure/mutation revokes local use and poisons the Owner, preventing subsequent valid scientific publication. No transport API is offered.

One necessary source seam is included as a copied `mcm_score_stream.py`: original finish can leave complete.json and closed=True after history/close failure. Such state is not a genuine success. The candidate captures original stream/batch directory identities from live FDs, rejoins after original history, and puts a private weak-key completion record only after close succeeds. The public read-only evidence helper requires that source-created record and unchanged original objects/fields/directories. The exact original constructor, matching/callback numerical loop, score/tail math, RNG, float order, original finish validation and close instructions are otherwise unchanged. `check_parity01.py` restores the whole baseline bytes/AST by removing the narrowly listed additions. No historical source is patched.

The adapter catches both concrete accepted local cleanup uncertainty and canonical production cleanup uncertainty by class identity; it does not equate arbitrary BaseException subclasses to successful cleanup. A foreign body primary is never passed into the content context's own reducer. Its exit is run with None arguments, independently, and the adapter reduces original and later errors chronologically, preserving the first actual fatal. This is distinct from suppressing failure: even when bytes are retained and content passes, an authority/close failure prevents a successful return.

`exact_members02.py` and `owned_io.py` are copied byte-for-byte from accepted reader02, including its ordinary mutation guard and first-fatal IO behavior. Scoretail02 is only an authenticated design dependency and remains untouched. Existing full tail/stage/matching verification is neither removed nor substituted; current adapter membership verification covers every original score-batch file. Full stream tails are not read as arrays, moved, deleted or authorized for retirement. Whole-run/financial science still require their genuine later checks.
