## RL1 — final storage failure has no durable failed observation

Location: launcher01.py:242–253, particularly the last root_watch at252 after the failure-record branch at245–251.

When no earlier failure exists, the wrapper writes observation.json with a non-null successful result and skips error.txt/failure.json. Its final root_watch can then detect a new disk-floor, file/count/type or allocated-storage violation; the code retains the exception and raises at254 but never attempts an additive failed/revoked observation. A disk-floor crossing between the earlier scan and final scan is sufficient; no malicious source or fabricated research receipt is needed.

The independent test extracts the exact original terminal-finally AST, uses an explicitly synthetic result (not an accepted experiment), lets the first watch pass and makes the second watch refuse. Actual observation.json survives with that result; failure.json and error.txt are absent; the selected exception would produce a nonzero exit. The wrapper therefore fails closed at the caller boundary, but its durable owned-tree evidence does not record the final failure or revoke its earlier observation. An external caller that loses the exit/traceback can recover only the misleading observation.

Required narrow correction: route the final-tail check into failure publication and preserve the original observation, using an exclusive additive failure/revocation marker or equivalent truthful terminal record. Bound the additional tail and account for its own storage, with no recursive self-hash promise. Failure of the additive publication must remain a failure and retain the original first actual fatal; neither overwriting the original observation nor converting final-watch failure into success is acceptable. Retain a regression for ordinary and fatal final-watch failure, failed marker publication, and an already-failed primary. The native helper, equations, limits, registration and source inventory need not change for this correction.
