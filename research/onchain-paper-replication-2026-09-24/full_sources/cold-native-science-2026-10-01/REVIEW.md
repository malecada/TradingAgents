## Initial findings and their resolution

The initial review withheld acceptance while the positive dictionary join failed
and two read-authority defects remained.

1. Dictionary configuration conventions differed from the accepted producer.
   The saved configuration contains the actual scalar backend, whereas the
   descriptor's `pair_execution` additionally wraps a policy hash. The saved
   matching identity hashes both matching configuration and backend. The final
   comparisons use those exact conventions; the representation binding's
   separate fitted configuration hash retains its original convention.
2. Declared NPY bytes alone did not bound numeric JSON-list conversion by
   `dictionary_from_record`. The final source calls the accepted pure dictionary
   `shape()` helper before `read_component` and decoding. It requires exact
   representative fields, native float64/int64 array slots, compatible dimensions
   and no unrelated arrays. Inline numeric fields cannot bypass the array cap.
3. Dictionary read limits were taken from sample-artifact policy without joining
   the dictionary publication's selected policy. The final source requires exact
   plan/job/proof/start dictionary-output input and hash agreement, validates its
   schema, checks retained metadata sizes, and intersects caller, sample-artifact
   and dictionary-output array/manifest/artifact limits.

These corrections close the reported issues. Metadata reads remain bounded by
the explicit global per-file/aggregate/count limits; the dictionary policy's
metadata-size validation occurs after those bounded reads. No stronger
before-read selected-policy claim is made here.
