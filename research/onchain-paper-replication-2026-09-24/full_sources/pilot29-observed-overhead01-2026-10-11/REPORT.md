The observed useful engineering target is the repeated authority callback route: it accounts for over half of this completed numeric prefix’s inclusive call time. That finding supports investigating avoidable implementation work inside the authorized callback while preserving its validation/revocation schedule and trusted scope. It does not authorize fewer polls, weaker checks or attribute the cost to a particular internal function without narrower evidence. The much smaller Binding phase totals do not establish that optimizing those four operations would remove the broad authority cost.

A normalization-only change cannot directly remove this measured callback time if call schedule and callback wall costs remain fixed. The residual5,087.199522070446s (46.6844281227%) contains all other timed memo work, including matching, cache/key operations, guards and checkpoint-related activity; it is NOT measured normalization time or solver CPU.
