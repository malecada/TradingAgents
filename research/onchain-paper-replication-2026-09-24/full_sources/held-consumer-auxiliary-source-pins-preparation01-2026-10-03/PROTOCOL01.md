Git commits. `_source_files` authenticates each experiment source pin at both
commits. Its `extension_metadata` requires each extension, allocation and review

confirmation or non-null selection-freeze routes; supporting those would require

with JSON budget/declaration files and a Markdown/text charter. All five paths
are unique, disjoint from199 implementation paths, other Root metadata roles and

wrong types/counts/hashes, source-map digest drift, and arbitrary extra source
pins refuse. The declaration cannot contain its own hash, current commit, or
registration hash. Root records its hash in registration.source_files externally.
