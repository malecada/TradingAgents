job payload. Execute the actual sampler, dictionary, MCM, native graph publication,
terminal preparation and proposed direction/regression executor in one temporary
registered fixture. The kernel guard is mocked; financial data and empirical fits
are excluded. Synthetic settings remain tiny, one epoch and batch size one.

Preserve previous failed checks and source snapshots. Each new execution has a
new identity; source is frozen through its process lifetime. No historical jobs
or empirical samples are replayed. First-owner admission rejects existing namespaces,
redirected ancestors and noncanonical graph sets. Incomplete construction is retained
and reported without inferred ownership or automatic recovery. Resident arrays are
explicitly loaded for this route; existing mapped/default routes remain separate.

Acceptance requires actual dispatch plus failure/refusal checks and independent
review. Synthetic success does not admit financial runs, demonstrate resource
capacity, cold/successor/mapped admission or complete the broader paper scope.
