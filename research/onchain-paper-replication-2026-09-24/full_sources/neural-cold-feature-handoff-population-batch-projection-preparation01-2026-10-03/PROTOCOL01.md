Only new candidate copies are owned. No live source, capsule, authority, registration, numeric body or empirical run is changed. Existing schema1 producer remains the default. Schema2 adds exactly projection_input and a fourth registered output named by outputs.projection. The selected path executes the unchanged original scientific assembler; no alternate eligibility, scaler, price lookup or model is implemented.

The added module contains a pure metadata projection and an actual ResearchRun publishing boundary. The latter must require exact ResearchRun type, source/registration/claim/input/output identity, actual loaded source locations and hashes, a registered projection policy/environment/model/training/component inventory, unchanged runtime inventory, and byte identity of all three original published outputs before and after publication. No truncation, data sampling, threshold tuning or source-cache shortcut.

Excluded rows are preserved, not reclassified.
