Known motifs have at most24 nodes/40 edges. For four node and two edge features, a conservative all32 motif-array payload bound is `32*(24*4*8 + 40*2*8 + 40*2*8)=65,536` bytes, excluding Python strings/objects, duplicate validation buffers and JSON bodies. Freeze per-graph census-derived limits, observed-vs-unknown sizes, scratch/checkpoint policy and cumulative reservation before the first large cell; never truncate/swap representatives to fit.
