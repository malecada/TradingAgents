# Storage publication observation correction

Candidate03 explicitly changes the sampled observation policy. Existing immutable publishers briefly create two links before unlinking scratch. A link observation discards that whole scan; at most two additional fresh scans may run. Every accepted complete scan still requires single-link regular files and the original fixed root identity. At most three times the configured per-scan entry bound may be visited. The one original scan deadline covers all scans, bounded waits and cleanup. Blocking filesystem calls are not preempted.

Observed quota breaches are never retried. Entries after an interrupted scan point remain unknown until a later scan; this is ordinary sampled non-atomic accounting, not proof that no byte breach or transient link existed. Persistent links, type/device/path/root failures and uncertain cleanup remain terminal. No file is removed or changed by the scanner. Every discarded link records bounded path, full-path hash, device/inode/nlink and that attempt's partial counters. Exact counts for unvisited entries are unknown.
