# Seven-graph topology preparation

This one-use engineering measurement determines complete weak-one-hop node
cardinalities for the seven already retained ETH weekly graphs selected by
pilot19. It is neither a replay of that failed pilot nor a financial experiment.
No dictionary sampling, clustering, motif matching, prediction, label access or
training occurs. The original pilot accounting and all failed records remain
unchanged. The separately declared identity is
`eth-seven-graph-weak-one-hop-census-20261008-01`.

The exact input list pins graph manifests, existing node-count receipts and
edge-index bodies. Node-ID headers corroborate dimensions; node-ID bodies,
features and labels are not decoded. Before/after body hashes and descriptor/path
stats establish observed input stability, not exclusion of concurrent writers.
The accepted census helper includes the center, deduplicates reciprocal/duplicate
neighbors, excludes self-loops from degree and never truncates a neighborhood.
Full count vectors are retained in at most 262144-element int64 NPY chunks.

The unchanged native guard enforces one unit with two CPUs, 1GiB memory.high and
memory.max, zero job swap, 600 seconds, 4MiB hard/soft writable-file limits,
2.5GiB startup/runtime host reserve and a 10GiB free-disk floor. Its sampled
storage watch covers the entire new owned working directory, including results,
guard receipts and native environment cache/temp locations, at 256MiB allocated
and logical bytes. The helper's explicit work-array allowance is 256MiB per
graph, excluding inputs, allocator and runtime overhead. This distinct topology
measurement does not increase the scientific pilot's memory cap.

Existing source/helper reviews are reused. One combined review binds the changed
caller, exact input list, settings, request and native entry. Preparation is
committed and remotely confirmed before Root launches once. Every attempted,
failed and unattempted graph is retained; existing output directories refuse a
second invocation. Actual elapsed/CPU time, qualified process RSS, native charged
memory and sampled storage remain distinct measures. Results receive independent
verification and incremental external preservation before dependent execution.
No capacity, throughput or completion claim for MCM/training follows from this
topology census alone. Its result sets the next complete-neighborhood and pair
workspace requirements; the 10000-node historical limit is not amended here.
