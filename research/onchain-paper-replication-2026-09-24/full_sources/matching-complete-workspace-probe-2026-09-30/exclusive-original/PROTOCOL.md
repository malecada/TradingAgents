# Complete synthetic matching resource probe

One new synthetic engineering identity measures the accepted isolated composite
matcher through the full frozen temperature schedule, complete greedy hardening,
scalar score-only finalization and checkpoint restoration. The fixture is a fresh
pair of identical 2,000-node, zero-edge graphs with constant zero node attributes.
Exactly 4,000,000 pair entries retains the existing capacity. The unchanged
matching-stable.json configuration is used; no two-iteration override applies.
This is neither a research claim nor a rerun of either closed prefix profile.

Retained state and each logical checkpoint are limited to 128 MiB, normalization
and hardening chunks to 65,536 entries, and hardening/scoring numeric scratch to
8 MiB each. All graph, validation, Python and runtime overhead is additionally
covered by the outer guard. After annealing, execute 256 hardening chunks, save
checkpoint01, delete only the in-memory state and restore it. Finish hardening,
score without dense assignment/result copies, then save and restore the completed
checkpoint02 and repeat only score finalization to check restored equivalence.
No second annealing or hardening execution occurs after completion.

Fixture checks are independent arithmetic invariants: the temperature loop takes
48 iterations with this configuration, uniform soft entries equal 1/2000 within
1e-14, the tie rule selects every diagonal pair, and the final zero-edge score is
0.5. Full hardening requires ceil(4,000,000/65,536) times (2,000+1) chunks, including
validation. These checks are not general scalar-oracle or accelerated parity.
The previous small scalar parity tests remain separate evidence.

One guard: 1 GiB maximum, 768 MiB high, zero swap, two CPUs, 3 GiB host reserve,
4 GiB startup memory, 10 GiB disk floor and 1,800 seconds. Launch only after
independent protocol/source review, fresh exact bindings/runtime/HEAD checks,
at least the disk floor plus 512 MiB free, and no active replication unit.
In particular, do not run alongside graph08, a saved-array verifier or storage.
Use exclusive guard, started, checkpoint and result paths. Preserve every failure
and partial; never retry this identity or increase limits following an outcome.
The two retained synthetic checkpoints occupy about 192 MB plus metadata; no
original raw, SQLite, graph array, financial input or remote body is read.

The profile measures zero-edge full-schedule matching at the current pair cap.
Nonzero edge cross-products, real hubs, full dictionaries, MCM, neural training,
GPU parity and financial performance remain unmeasured. It does not integrate a
new production backend, increase capacity, amend empirical budgets or admit fits.
