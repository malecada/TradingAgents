# Imported MCM control-overhead investigation

The existing genuine imported route can execute without archive transport, but it cannot currently execute without the compact per-pair audit format. Raising storage budgets or reducing the JSON allowance alone does not remove its dominant validation and synchronization work. The smallest correction is explicitly selected chunk durability plus bounded archive controls, retaining all original event bytes and the same engine. A route that omits those event bytes would require a separate coupled Owner/publication contract, not a callback substitution.

## Concrete overhead

1. **Repeated closed-history scans:** `archive_dispatch.py:284` (`Context._outer`) inventories and rereads every `_expected` control; `_diagnostics` at line 320 reopens and hashes every retained diagnostic. `_live:348` calls `_outer` twice, and low transport invokes liveness during command polling. `_call:363` scans diagnostics again on completion. With one history scan per command, 472,000 commands already entail 111,392,236,000 prefix-item visits; actual callbacks amplify this. Successful payload staging may move away, but diagnostic metadata and dispatch records accumulate. This is quadratic history work, not matching.
2. **Per-event Ledger history scans:** `archive_owner_writer.py:65` routes the writer lease into `Ledger._live`. `compact_pair_log.py:110–157` calls that lease repeatedly for every event. `archive_owner_operations.py:89–127` always rereads the complete ledger, including `typed_payload_operations.py:240`'s entire typed-control history. Optimizing Context alone leaves this growing per-cell cost intact.
3. **Synchronous per-cell tracing:** `compact_pair_log.py:152` fsyncs each begin/complete event; `score_tail.py:121` fsyncs each returned score. Successful May9 would therefore require at least 217,486,176 such calls, excluding directories, checkpoints and other metadata. Transport commands occur per chunk/operation, **not literally once per distance**. Per-distance binary forensic events and their synchronous acknowledgements are a separate cost.
4. **Conservative admission arithmetic:** the withheld 309,339,357,184-byte control/diagnostic allowance is reconstructed in ARITHMETIC01. It is a maximum selected-envelope charge, not measured disk consumption. The calculation applies an eight-stage/16-reader bound; imported dictionary completion actually performs zero current matching pairs. Correct per-stage denominators would help, but cannot cure the history scans or synchronous pair logging.

## Smallest concrete correction

Keep the actual imported kernel, CompactMatcher engine/order, PairLog and score-tail payload formats, full fresh recovery and genuine Owner identities. Add one explicit versioned resource-only durability/control selection, with legacy behavior unchanged:

- Split live checks from closed-history audits in Context and Ledger. Live checks retain the genuine active Run/Owner/stage/held transition, native lease, current command/claim, immutable object/policy identity and monotonic spent counters. Authenticate new closed controls once at publication. Audit closed history at explicit finite selected time/call boundaries and mandatory stage/pre-publication/final boundaries. Record the delayed mutation-detection assumption; a cached hash is not writer exclusion. Failure cannot refresh freshness or refund counters. Typed ledger evidence needs the same separation.
- Replace one-file-per-success control/diagnostic expansion with finite append-only success-control shards and exact byte/entry charges. Preserve command ordinal, kind, reservation, exit/cleanup, payload/remote identity and diagnostic digest. Retain complete bounded stderr/partial-body/cleanup evidence for failures; reserve failure space before launching a command. Change the low transport's success diagnostic writer as well as Context's consumer, otherwise old files still accumulate. Never erase old receipts or silently compact a historical run.
- Derive prospective control charges from actual per-stage commands/shard schema, with explicit failure allowance; update the allocation calculator and registration together. Keep data full-get and scientific replay obligations independent. This is a finite change to dispatch/transport, ledger/typed evidence and their policy arithmetic, not a new launcher.

- Add selected chunk/time-bounded durability at `compact_pair_log.py:152` and `score_tail.py:121`: retain every original event, f64 bit, ordinal, purpose and checksum, and retain physical write/pread acknowledgements; defer fsync to explicit finite boundaries. Flush before rotating/closing a chunk, checkpoint publication, tail/batch seal, remote snapshot, normal failure terminal and final publication. `compact_matcher._ack_check:182` can continue checking the same physical bytes. It must not call a buffered acknowledgement crash-durable. Retain and report the last successfully flushed boundary separately from current computed counts; a killed/unflushed suffix is unavailable, not a completed batch. Flush errors remain primary/secondary fatal evidence and cannot advance that durable boundary. This requires selected-policy plumbing through PairLog/tail/stream and their start/terminal validators, not a replacement matching implementation.

The genuine `compact_owner._finish_stage:351` already calls `compact_stage.seal/verify` over actual logs. Retain full semantic replay at those finite publication/final boundaries; do not substitute caller counters. The same resource-retained and output publication checks must recognize the selected durability contract while retaining exact full-cell completion requirements. Failed partial work remains zero representation/financial credit. Closed-history caching must keep source/Owner/current-operation bindings and exact inode/size/mode/mtime/ctime observations; these fingerprints cannot detect all same-size restored-timestamp mutations. Indefinite reuse would need enforceable writer exclusion not demonstrated here. Finite full-byte checks require an explicit sampled detection-latency assumption, not an invented immutability grant.

The immediately implementable slice is the selected flush policy plus the two growing-history hot paths and bounded successful control encoding. Required focused checks are exact event/tail byte parity on success, interrupted-prefix accounting, flush failure retaining the original exception, stale/revoked/mutated authority refusal, and actual encoded success/failure bounds. These changes leave matching scheduling, motifs, samples, all graph nodes/edges, full replay and model gradients untouched. They do not prove eight-hour completion or whole-pilot capacity.
