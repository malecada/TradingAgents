# Deferred lease subphase instrumentation — paired source candidate

Uninstalled paired candidate for compact_mcm.py and real_pilot_partial_progress.py. Exact current Main baselines, forward/inverse diffs and hashes are retained. No native22/source-freeze/registration/budget/resource policy changes occurred.

Three finite timing keys are appended: lease_authority, lease_start_metadata, lease_progress. The private `_measure_lease_subphase` and `_record_lease_subphase` only validate/accumulate timing. They never invoke `_write`, `_record`, a callback refresh or a checkpoint. The existing outer `measure('lease', ...)` retains its original refresh opportunity after the entire lease body, including on an original exception. Existing measure and _record function ASTs are identical to baseline.

The diagnostic-enabled lease executes the original imported/legacy authority branch exactly once, followed by the original producer root/read/current-JSON require exactly once, then the original poll exactly once when progress exists. The diagnostic-None branch retains its original `_lease_body` unchanged. No extra target/guard/graph/interval callback is introduced. Deferred counts are cumulative and become visible on the next existing diagnostic publication; they are not separate authority records. Outer lease is inclusive and includes instrumentation overhead; subphase totals must not be added to it.

## Verification and limits

TEST02 is the controlling receipt:56 focused callback cases compare baseline and candidate actual AST-extracted lease functions with stdlib-only fakes. Imported/legacy paths, present/absent progress, diagnostic enabled/None, success and failures in target/stage/dictionary/root/read/JSON/mismatch/poll retain original operation ordering. No later original operation runs after failure. The original exception object is preserved. Each enabled outer call has exactly one refresh at the end and no intermediate write. Private result forwarding, invalid finite-key refusal and preservation of the primary callback exception when timing also fails are checked.

All three deferred keys are fixed, not a general public profiling interface. Private timing has two additional monotonic reads per executed span: four per successful no-progress lease; six with progress. A failure during authority adds two; a failure during start-metadata adds four; a failure during progress adds six. No-progress leases do not increment the progress counter. Existing outer timing reads remain unchanged. Added `_check` and clock validations can fail at new diagnostic boundaries; a nonfinite initial subphase clock is explicitly shown to prevent that callback. New accumulator corruption/overflow can also stop a later original operation. Therefore callback/error traces are equivalent for healthy diagnostic state/clock, **not universally identical for invalid clocks or corrupted diagnostic state**. An additional timing failure during a callback exception attaches a note and preserves the primary exception.

Checkpoint stress uses all28 startup records, the three additional counters, large finite seconds/count values,64-character source/claim/graph identities and a64-character failure name. The real publication method writes6329B, below the existing8192B cap. No cap increased. This focused stress is not a universal maximum for arbitrary malformed diagnostic objects.

Final execution asserts CPUaffinity0–1, nice10,256MiB address-space and4MiB RLIMIT_FSIZE, under a60s shell timeout; NumPy is not imported. TEST01 also passed functional cases but its shell file-block value8192 represented8MiB on Bash. That run and artifacts remain preserved; TEST02 corrected the value to4096 and asserted the exact4MiB limit. No failing evidence was discarded and no full suite/MCM/array workload ran.

The source candidate introduces small local wrappers and duplicate exact authority/metadata statements to keep the original diagnostic-None body intact. It does not cache authority, source bytes, graph identities or validation results. Independent paired review and Root integration remain required before any empirical entry. No throughput improvement is claimed; this patch produces the evidence needed to choose the next optimization.
