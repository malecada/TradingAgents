Accepted as a bounded requirements investigation and conditional arithmetic comparison. No retention policy is adopted, no artifact disposal is authorized, and no physical feasibility or integration acceptance follows. No blocker was found in that narrow conclusion.

That absence is not itself permission to delete anything.

The operative constraints are stronger than the paper alone: current implementation assumptions explicitly preserve progress/failure snapshots; current compact and archived stage verifiers join every referenced checkpoint tree and every score tail. Existing historical and current-variant artifacts cannot be retrospectively reclassified.

A bounded future restart-retention design is not inherently incompatible with the unchanged equations or C01–C18 objectives.
