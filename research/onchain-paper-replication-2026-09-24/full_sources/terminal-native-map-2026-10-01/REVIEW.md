## Findings and implementation

For **NM1**, minimum capacity is computed from every required resident graph's node/edge dimensions and the actual motif count before `seal.finish`. The largest payload must fit the live-wrapper cap, and twice that payload plus bounded chunk scratch must fit the numeric cap. Pure boundary tests cover each limit separately. The closed registered low-cap fixture forbids the sealer and confirms refusal with no numerical seal or seal-attempt directory. This establishes individual-graph feasibility at zero prior live returns; it does not guarantee that every multi-graph batch fits.

For **NM2**, public live-byte accounting now uses the same nonblocking lock as loading and verification. Internal pruning occurs within those locked operations. The saved thread/barrier test confirms that a query during an active batch refuses, new returned tensors remain counted afterward, and a subsequent excess allocation refuses. The old lost-registration interleaving itself was not reproduced; the corrected exclusion contract was tested. Allowing final accounted bytes to decrease accommodates older wrappers being released during an operation.

The registered preparation route selects policy through both plan and execution job and calls the actual reviewed sealer once. References derive from that returned transition, its proved graph closure and exact published binding; an arbitrary Receipt or private helper construction does not grant admission. The subclass overrides iteration, item access, loading and verification, retaining both maintained evaluator dispatches while avoiding the generic ArrayReference/mmap path.

Loading checks unknown keys and deduplicates membership before allocation. The strict reader verifies the exact native tree, shape/type/order, extents and stored hashes; the accepted boundary creates independent CPU tensors and verifies numerical wire identity, including empty edges. The operation cap includes prior tracked wrappers, all new unique tensor payloads, the largest current native payload and chunk scratch. Sequential `verified_hashes` recomputes content identity with its own read/scratch bound and prior-live allowance. Full terminal/content leases bracket operations; active run/guard checks remain inside strict reads and conversion. Late failure clears new results.

## Scope exclusions

Live accounting covers original returned tensor wrappers only. Detached aliases, views or autograd-held storage can outlive those wrappers; their exclusion prevents interpreting this as a bound on all resident feature storage. Model activations/state, resident parent graphs and dictionary/sample prerequisites, Python metadata, stream buffers, page cache and total RSS are also outside the local cap. Returned tensors are mutable model inputs; layout/storage checks do not constitute immutability of caller-held values. The outer guard and separate whole-workflow resource admission remain required.

Kernel observations are mocked in registered synthetic fixtures. The model smoke test uses tiny synthetic features and forward/backward gradients, without an optimizer update or financial fit. No full registered executor, cold/historical reuse, mapped parent storage, arbitrary-batch feasibility, scalable full-fold lease/I/O performance, financial timing/accounting, fees/funding, empirical coverage, trial-budget totals or paper agreement was verified. No empirical launch is authorized by this acceptance.
