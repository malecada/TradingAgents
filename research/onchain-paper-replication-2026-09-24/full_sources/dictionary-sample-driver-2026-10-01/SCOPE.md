# Dictionary execution from an admitted current sample publication

This isolated driver composes the accepted current-owner sample-artifact route,
pair consumer and complete dictionary workload. The only supplied values are the
actual OwnedJournal and FeatureJournal plus the explicit registered artifact input
and first-event reference. Matching settings, dictionary settings, workflow, backend
and matrix entry cap come from the admitted route. The caller cannot submit a
score callback, arbitrary purpose or replacement numerical settings.

Before execution, direct source bytes must match the registration at both the
import checkout and admitted run root. The actual published sample event is
admitted and its induced neighborhoods validated. The accepted dictionary workload
internally derives each purpose. Every pair callback checks sample receipt,
registered source and owned-journal leases before and after the real serial
consumer. Its scope must match the admitted sample scope. A final lease occurs
after clustering and before returning the dictionary and diagnostic matrices.

This is current first-owner resident composition, not full producer release.
The result deliberately retains empirical_admission_verified=false. The driver
does not publish a dictionary artifact or claim sampler draw/RNG provenance.
