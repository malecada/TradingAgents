## Material qualification

The original README, builder prerequisite `floor_reconciliation`, and physical worksheet describe a 20 GiB plus workspace requirement as applying to the current sampler generally. This is overbroad. `compact_sampler._prepare` selects `resident-leased-v1` and requires `16 * centers <= max_direct_weight_bytes`; the pinned leased core allocates resident float64 weights and probabilities. It does not enter MappedWeights. `neighborhoods.sample_neighborhoods` selects MappedWeights only when `weight_workspace` is supplied, and that optional path retains the 20 GiB plus reserved workspace requirement in `sampling_weights.py`.

The prospective route must distinguish these paths. The resident route requires direct-vector and additional NumPy/index/neighborhood/parent/aggregate RSS accounting; the optional mapped route retains its stricter disk check. The authorized 10 GiB floor is not relaxed. An unselected mapped-path requirement must not be presented as a blocker for the resident compact route. The author has been notified and is preserving the original before correction.
