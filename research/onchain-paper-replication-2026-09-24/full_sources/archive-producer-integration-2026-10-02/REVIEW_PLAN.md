# Independent early integration contract and coverage review

This is an in-progress source and test-design review, not acceptance. The integration source is still changing; no integrated execution result was inspected and no test or network operation was run by the reviewer. Ownership is limited to this note. The contract and current four-test draft were read alongside the dictionary, MCM, owner, terminal and archive wrapper changes.

## Material implementation concerns sent to the author

1. **Original numerical authority must precede archive callbacks.** In the current `compact_dictionary._produce_locked` draft, `compute` returns `kernel.fit` output to `archive_owner_writer._run_locked`, which finishes the event archive and completes its reservation through live callbacks. Only after it returns does the producer derive its payload fingerprint. The former local path pinned that output before log completion/seal callbacks. A late callback can therefore change a distance matrix or dictionary object before the new baseline is established. Capture the original numerical fingerprint inside `compute`, before returning to the writer wrapper, and verify it after wrapper return. The MCM draft similarly moves `stream.finish` ahead of the original matrix pin; restore the original ordering even though the subsequent exact saved-score comparison supplies an additional refusal boundary. A regression should arm on writer completion, earlier than the currently tested stage-seal callback.

2. **Finally checks must not downgrade fatal cleanup.** New unconditional `held.check(owner)` calls in the `finally` clauses of `archive_owner_writer._run_locked`, `archive_owner_stage._execute_locked` and `compact_mcm._produce_locked` can replace an in-flight CleanupFailure with an ordinary ValueError if the token or owner lock also changed. Preserve fatal cleanup as the propagated failure, chaining or noting the invalid transition. The outer `_held` context must release its captured original lock once regardless of replacement. A small isolated test can cover fatal-plus-invalid-token without repeating an expensive scientific fixture.

## Acceptance cases missing or insufficiently discriminating in the initial draft

- The positive full route forbids remote methods after closure and calls terminal full checks. Add a negative that changes an archived stage seal, claim or v2 proof after closure, including a coherently rehashed replacement where appropriate, and requires rejection against the original producer/terminal anchors. Successful local-only replay alone does not establish original proof authority.
- The exact top-level journal inventory check and foreign-directory negative are useful. Include a deterministic read-attempt or ledger namespace replacement/dangling failure marker case, or explicitly retain prior equivalent evidence. Allowed names must continue to derive from actual admitted claim membership; observed directory names must never extend admission.
- Dictionary `actual <= capacity` permits equality and does not demonstrate the intended reused-subset denominator. Use a deterministic known reuse fixture or separately bind existing count-contract evidence, assert the exact actual count and its strict inequality where claimed, and check aggregate owner pairs equal dictionary actual plus exact MCM cells. MCM assertions should derive the expected count from graph rows and ordered motifs, not solely compare two fields populated by the same implementation.
- Duplicate writer/read claim or second producer invocation is not tested in this draft. A refusal must preserve successful claim bytes and spent counters, create no extra remote object/read attempt and leave the previously completed evidence intact. Differentiate finite additional reader allowances from an illicit duplicate writer or owner reopen.
- The late MCM mutation test checks a writer completion, one reader count and the stage marker. Also pin the successful reader completion bytes and reservation totals before injecting the failure, assert those remain preserved, and require absence of false overall representation success. This distinguishes retained spent evidence from a repaired/reissued claim.
- Exercise the new internal token boundary without another full chain where possible: expired token, wrong thread or owner/lock replacement, and nested public entry refusal. Scientific and operation leases must both remain live through numerical computation and archive publication.

The planned actual archive chain and unchanged-local-route case are necessary. Existing focused archive writer/stage/seal tests can supply explicitly identified negative evidence; every case need not repeat the whole producer. The coupled full-chain result still must demonstrate dictionary, every required MCM stage, saved output, closure/publication, owner and representation terminal, followed by local-only full content checks under terminal authority.

## Scope retained

The new callback-free `check_content` is a content verifier requiring an externally anchored seal/contract. It cannot itself admit a live owner or establish current remote availability. Active wrappers must bind actual ledger and operations; post-close wrappers must use the original terminal phase authority without bypassing old active-owner checks. No caller-completed reference alone can replace the full reserved archive read used for stage sealing.

All proposed fixtures are synthetic with filesystem transport and inherited mocked guard. This review supplies no real SSH, resource-capacity, full calendar/price validity, financial outcome, full-workflow throughput or empirical admission claim. Final acceptance awaits frozen source and closed integrated evidence.
