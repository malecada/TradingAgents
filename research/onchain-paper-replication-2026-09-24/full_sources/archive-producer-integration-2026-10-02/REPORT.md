# Injected archive producer-to-terminal integration

The explicit registered archive extension is reconstructed from the admitted
policy hash in both native producer selection and training. The native producer
accepts a keyword-only transport, selects the finite archive policy and attaches
its ledger before training and the first stage. Dictionary and MCM producers
retain their owner transition through explicit private archive writer/read bodies.
An immutable token captures the exact lock, thread and active context lifetime;
public archive wrappers still acquire their own transitions. Scientific leases
are composed with claim liveness. Numerical fingerprints are pinned before
writer/stream completion callbacks and rechecked afterward.

Archived dictionary, MCM, saved output and owner completion share a callback-free
local content verifier. The trusted stage seal binds original ledger/claim inodes,
intent hashes, completion references and the anchored v2 scientific read proof.
Current object wrappers additionally join actual operation membership. Output
arguments carry these original pins under existing producer/output references.
Terminal checks retain their existing run/source/guard phase authority after
owner closure; there is no owner-validation bypass. Exact allowed journal names
derive from admitted writer/read operations. Successful owner completion closes
the finite ledger while preserving its consumed reservations.

## Scope boundary

This is an **in-process injected transport route**. The registered outer
dispatcher in `job_payload.py` still calls the native producer without a
transport. Dispatcher configuration, actual SSH integration and payload metering
are not connected by this slice. Tests use only synthetic graphs, temporary
registrations, filesystem transport and the fixture's mocked OS guard. No
empirical job, remote transfer, authentication-file read or historical rerun
occurred. No resource/physical-storage sufficiency or financial claim follows.

The default local route is preserved. Existing scientific kernels, owner source
checks, admission gates and registered count rules remain. Ledger payload figures
remain conservative logical allowances, not actual wire bytes or physical storage.
Post-close content checks establish retained local consistency, not current
remote availability. Historical old seals are not upgraded in place.
