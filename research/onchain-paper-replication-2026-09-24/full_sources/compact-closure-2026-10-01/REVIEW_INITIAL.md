Acceptance withheld for CCL1. Review covers `compact_closure.py.draft` and the initial synthetic test source only. No module was installed, test executed, or empirical data read by the reviewer.

## CCL1 — final callback can revoke the owner outside the final evidence checks

`Receipt.lease()` invokes dictionary, denominator and graph publication leases. A wrapper around the last graph lease can first run the original lease, then poison the owner or add a compact/representation terminal marker or foreign owner-root entry. The following `_final()` does not repeat the equivalent callback-free owner admission checks. `_membership()` requires no active stage and `not owner.closed`, but does not reject `owner.poisoned`. `_verified_stages()` checks each stage's artifacts, not the owner root or representation terminal state. Remaining original-feature and publication evidence checks likewise do not restore this owner boundary.

Consequently a metadata closure can be returned after its owner was revoked by the last external callback, despite the stated current-owner scope. Required correction: callback-free final verification of exact Binding/run authority, owner configuration/reservation, owner root inode and owner.json, exact root membership, and compact/representation terminal-marker absence. Match existing owner boundary semantics and reject broken terminal symlinks. Adding another external live lease alone moves rather than closes the gap. A synthetic regression should inject revocation/terminal or foreign-root state after the last graph lease delegates successfully and require refusal.
