# Compact complete graph/calendar binding result

The maintained compact_closure adapter admits the actual same-Training dictionary,
complete calendar denominator and exact unique required graph artifact population.
Every saved graph payload is loaded and released sequentially, with full original
ancestry, completed stage and saved artifact verification. The resulting schema3
binding preserves fixed graph hashes, dictionary, configuration, fold, training
membership, source/time/node/edge lineage and full denominator metadata.

The callback-free current-owner check resolves CCL1 from REVIEW_INITIAL.md. The
new source keeps full cross-graph checks after each graph readback; the proposed
scalar-only optimization was deferred and is not claimed.

## Evidence

- red01.log: 1 missing-module failure, 1 deselected, 2.69s; session51823 exit1.
- check01.log: 2 passed, 200.42s; session43581 exit0.

The actual registered synthetic fixture contains two required graphs and twenty
calendar days. It checks normal closure/readback, missing and duplicate graph
refusal, then patches the final Receipt.lease to invoke its original successfully
and poison the owner. Subsequent check rejects that revoked owner. The older
retained test-red01.py used a different corruption injection and is not the final
regression. The pure unowned-caller test refuses arbitrary objects.

## Limits

No durable representation event, owner terminal, output registry publication,
cold reuse or native fit handoff is produced. The numeric reservation counts
retained source arrays, original feature tensors and one loaded graph. Other
parent attributes, dictionary/sample storage, provenance readbacks, I/O/Python
scratch, earlier retained Loaded values, caller-expanded backing storage and RSS
remain separately accountable. Complete calendar membership is verified; price
exclusion reconstruction remains outstanding. These are synthetic checks with
mocked guards, not a financial experiment or full-size resource pilot.
