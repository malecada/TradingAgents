# Independent final review

Accepted for bounded current-owner compact graph/calendar closure into an in-memory metadata binding. CCL1 is resolved by the callback-free current-owner verification. `REVIEW_INITIAL.md` remains preserved. No tests, empirical jobs, or historical numerical replay were run by the reviewer; source, current test wiring, raw terminal evidence and hashes were independently inspected.

The implementation requires the actual Produced dictionary, the denominator receipt for the exact same Training object, and one actual Published artifact for every required graph with no duplicates or omissions. Each artifact must retain that dictionary and owner. The owner must have exactly the required closed dictionary/MCM stages and no active stage. Full checks rejoin the dictionary/sample lineage, registered daily denominator, all stage evidence, each original graph/feature and saved artifact, and the exact graph-artifact parent inventory.

Saved graph tensors are loaded sequentially through each publication's strict current-owner loader, checked and explicitly released before the next load. Full cross-graph `_final` checks remain after every load as well as at entry and after the last result lease. The proposed optimization was not installed or accepted. The final schema-3 binding includes required feature hashes and both required-graph and training-only dictionary lineage, graph source/availability/node/edge ordering, fold/train/configuration identity and the empty alignment order appropriate to motif MCM.

CCL1 originally allowed a final external lease to revoke the owner after the last owner check. `_final` now invokes `compact_owner.verify_current`, which checks pinned Binding/run/metadata, claim and terminal state, first-owner exclusivity, compact root identity/inventory/configuration/reservation and stage intents without another user/Owner/Binding/guard lease. Artifact original-state checks use the same helper. A fresh live guard is still supplied by the preceding lease; the overall boundary is sampled rather than atomic.

No new numerical estimator is introduced. The initial missing-module red run is infrastructure evidence only. Sequential object release was inspected in source; this suite does not independently measure weak-reference collection, peak RSS, every policy-cap branch, every saved member mutation or full-size performance.

## Boundaries

The selected allowance `2*sum(graph payloads)+max(graph payload)` covers already retained counted source/features and one newly loaded graph payload. It excludes previously retained Loaded receipts, parent attributes beyond those payloads, dictionary/sample arrays, provenance readbacks, verification/I/O scratch, Python objects, model state, expanded/aliased caller tensor storage and process RSS. Logical bounds and tiny execution do not establish full-workflow physical storage/runtime feasibility.

The daily denominator includes explicit price exclusions without independently recomputing them or validating prices/labels. This closure does not publish a representation event, seal the owner, publish ResearchRun outputs, enable native dispatch or cold/successor reuse, or admit empirical fitting. The owner remains active, and subsequent publication/handoff needs its own reviewed contract.
