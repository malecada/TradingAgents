# Prospective price-source amendment after transport denial

Both registered Yahoo requests ended with HTTP429. Each retained all3289 source
cells, zero admitted prices, immutable response bytes and verified process
cleanup. No real predictive fit or accuracy comparison exists. Their closed
identities, original calendar and source gates remain unchanged.

The prospective source is Coin Metrics' own community CSV archive, pinned to
commit f1a36afb962731c387bb03982758ab0103063da5. Only the `PriceUSD` column is a
candidate. The provider defines its daily metric as the USD price at UTC day-end,
computed from its reference-rate methodology. The archive generator writes the
ISO date portion of the API time; the date denotes the represented day, with
availability assumed at next-day midnight. No ReferenceRateUSD field, shifted
calendar, interpolation or provider/date blending is permitted. Historic release
latency and original paper data identity remain unrecovered.

This changes the price provider/methodology explicitly; it is an independent
replication assumption, not author-exact data. All original seven rolling folds,
28-day lookback, labels, neural models, seeds,1420 unique fit cells and88,776 source-field cells are retained.
Actual CSV coverage/schema is UNKNOWN before the registered capture. Missing or
invalid dates remain visible; no test outcome selects a provider or setting.

Two new source identities are proposed, one BTC and one ETH. Each may request
its pinned public CSV once, no retry, redirect, proxy, credentials or alternate
URL. Required date cells remain2016-01-01 through2024-12-31. Other archive rows
are retained in the raw response but are excluded from model inputs and label
construction; no new predictor uses other metrics. A full response/parser bound
and exact gate must be frozen and independently reviewed before retrieval.

The cumulative ceiling remains51 and22 claims are already consumed, including
the active pilot and both Yahoo failures. The new allocation reserves four price
claims in total and twelve missing-body claims, retaining seventeen source claims.
Unlaunched BTC2016–2019 body dates are grouped into one finite source batch;
BTC2020–2024 and ETH2016–2021 retain their declared remaining batches. This changes
batch boundaries only, never the required dates/fields, resource limits or fit
population. No family history is renamed/reset and no admission helper is changed.
A separate higher ceiling would need an explicit extension mechanism and must not
invalidate an active claim. No such increase is requested or executed here.
