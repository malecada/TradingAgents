## Bounded case and counterconditions

For a proposed singleton-only numerical memoization proof, require both left neighborhoods have exactly one node and zero edges (a singleton with a self-loop is not isolated), exact numeric body/dtype/shape equality, valid individual typed contracts, unchanged ordered motif arrays, same left/right orientation, effective config, operation/chunk schedule, implementation and numerical runtime. Revalidate each occurrence's original identities/authority independently; an old scalar is not current admission or checkpoint evidence. Preserve original rejection, convergence/iteration tuple, scalar float64 bits and final float32 conversion. Do not replace the solver with “best matching node”: stable ranking and one-pass rectangular normalization are the actual method.

Counterconditions include different singleton features, reordered motif rows/edges, different orientation/config/runtime, nonzero self-loops, or invalid individual graph identity. Merely “one node”, “isolated”, equal mathematical values after dtype conversion, or isomorphism is insufficient for this exact-body case.

Required future proof is an independently reviewed deterministic numerical differential check against fresh genuine engine create/advance/score_only for separately valid labelled fixtures: compare assignment ordering, convergence/iterations, float64 scalar bytes and final float32 bytes, including ties, underflow/zero agreement and refusal cases. Separately prove per-occurrence log, retention, diagnostic and failure semantics remain honest. Skipping solver work cannot silently claim the original execution/checkpoint trace. Those checks and any empirical frequency/cost measurement are outside this task. No graph bodies, numerical imports/tests, native jobs, claims, or source edits were made; no speedup/population-frequency claim is supported.
