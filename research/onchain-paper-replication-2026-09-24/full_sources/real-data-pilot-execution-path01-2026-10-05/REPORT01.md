Concrete blockers and minimal implementation

1. **Raw-to-fit boundary.** Current fit payload does not call the graph producer; both population and graph loading consume already admitted input manifests (`job_payload.py:98–118,234`; `population_assembly.py:164`). If genuine seven-week graph artifacts already exist and raw aggregation is outside the measured pilot, register their exact bodies and reuse. If raw aggregation must be inside this one pilot, add a fixed in-run handoff accepting only graph outputs actually published by this run. `registered_features.py:70–77` already demonstrates the correct published-output hash check. Do not invent graph hashes or retrofit admission after numerical work.
2. **Original imported dictionary is not Main's fit producer.** Main currently lacks original_dictionary/resource_binding/import-stage modules, while the accepted canonical capsule has them. Its `resource_fixture.py:267` hardcodes tiny-target limits and produces resource-only MCM terminals (`financial_representation_admitted=False`), not the full training PreparedFeatures handoff. Reuse that reviewed source/typed factory in a new explicitly scoped real-data pilot route, then add a bounded adapter from actual imported-MCM matrices plus complete edges to the model. Preserve the old fixture untouched; do not turn its terminal into scientific-fit authority.
3. **One batch is not a completed fit.** Neither generic fit nor the observer limits the workload to the selected16 rows. `training_batch_observer.py:174–209` only observes chosen call ordinals (up to32) and explicitly disclaims complete-population census. Register the actual16 rows,28 dates, seven graph hashes, real labels/prices, original32 motif evidence, model/optimizer settings and exactly one resource update in the new pilot plan. Execute a finite step/checkpoint via genuine run/guard/Owner; record resource completion, never claim100-epoch fit completion. Do not shorten or amend an existing financial cell.
4. **Timing boundaries.** Existing guard gives whole-job timing/peaks, and training checkpoints give state/cursor, but the full ordinary graph→MCM→fit path lacks a unified phase timer. Add only phase records around raw aggregation/load, population, dictionary import, each MCM, tensor preparation, forward/backward/optimizer and checkpoint/readback. Use monotonic timing and clearly scoped last cgroup kernel peaks; do not derive per-phase maxima from unrelated ancestor peaks or claim a final peak after losing the cgroup.
