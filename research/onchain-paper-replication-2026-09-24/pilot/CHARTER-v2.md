Objective: measure the frozen transaction-graph replication pipeline with retained
ETH sources and synthetic training labels. No predictive outcome, tuning, price
capture, old-run replay or original source mutation is permitted. The previous17
claims remain consumed. This new engineering attempt remains one of the proposed
34 additive allowances; the separate metadata claim has since completed (cumulative ceiling51); any interrupted retry needs a new
reviewed allowance/identity. This pilot is not a financial strategy test.

The eight fixed weeks and largest complete training week by declared transaction
count are bound by source-index.json. Exact source bodies are verified during the
new transform, not refetched. January1,2024 uses its explicitly mapped compressed
context ranges and original base64 footer receipt. Metadata mapping-v1's failed

Resource limits: protocol-freeze-v4,3GiB memory/2.5GiB high/zero swap, the same two
CPUs for all threads,3GiB additional host reserve,20GiB disk floor, at most8hours

last progress and exact unmet scope; dependent phases remain unavailable, while
independent graph/resource phases continue. No neighborhood, dictionary or graph

Nine graph-build/decode phases use every transaction in their declared weeks.
Neighborhood sampling uses512 centers/seed11. A resource-only32-motif dictionary

neighborhoods paired with the next32 as a fixed timing batch; this is a component
probe, not a replacement for full MCM. Full MCM attempts every node against all32
motifs and retains any incomplete prefix distinctly. GAT/full-model resource
probes use the actual complete weekly topology and synthetic MCM inputs, prices
and labels; their results establish tensor cost only, not matching coverage.

capacity forecasts explicitly multiply the per-graph incremental peak by the
observed/calendar-required unique-week bound and apply1.5margin. That estimate is
not a demonstrated multiweek batch. Checkpoint I/O writes the actual probe model
and optimizer. All source, decode/build, neighborhood, matching, dictionary, MCM,
neural and checkpoint cells—including unavailable dependencies—remain in ledger.

Outputs: source/phase cell ledger, immutable artifact index, measured time/memory
receipts and capacity forecast. Original raw plus new graphs/features/checkpoints,
temporary peak, per-fold/seed repetition and backup requirements are accounted for.
At insufficient capacity, remaining financial fits stay pending and independently

contact requires separate authorization. This charter alone cannot admit a run.
