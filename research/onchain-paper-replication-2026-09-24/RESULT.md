## Resource and coverage limits

Resource pilot 02 is **terminal failed**, with all 109 cells retained: 7 complete and 102 unavailable. During the largest-week graph build, systemd-oomd killed eight owned processes because ancestor-user memory pressure exceeded its threshold for more than 20 seconds. The local journal identifies this cause; zero kernel cgroup OOM counters do not mean there was no memory-pressure kill. The guard recorded 10,247.694 seconds, a sampled peak of 2,783,363,072 bytes and verified cleanup. Independent verification checked all 69 indexed artifact hashes and all 109 registered cell identities. The 63 source-day ledger cells retain their post-interruption unavailable status, while completed graphs prove 14 decoded days and a third week was attempted; unavailable does not mean unexposed. No process or claim is relaunched. First-week dictionary construction completed 512 samples and 32 motifs. First and second full-node MCM attempts refused a neighborhood over the frozen 10,000-node capacity; no truncation was used. Neural phases refused conservative intermediate estimates of 10.713 GB and 8.779 GB before forward under the 1 GiB working allowance. These estimates are not measured minimum RAM or proof of intrinsic algorithmic infeasibility.

Under the current uncompressed graph/MCM artifact format, the reviewed two-week storage extrapolation projects 80.893 GB of weekly graphs and 190.563 GB of five-seed MCM arrays for 156 initial ETH weeks: 407.184 GB after the 1.5 planning margin. Additional raw data, samples, models, scratch, other folds/comparators and backup are excluded. Root/Data free space above their 20 GiB floors was about 6.39 GB/2.50 GB. The largest-week graph did not finish, so a complete worst-case forecast is unavailable. No full-source acquisition or fit is released on these estimates.
