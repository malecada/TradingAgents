## Evidence ladder and provisional numerical standards

1. Source/data feasibility and cashflow-definition diagnostics. These can pass
   integrity checks without passing an economic gate.
2. Development mechanism and conditional economics. An annualized simple net
   cash rate of at least 3% of *all committed capital*, after actual modeled
   costs and reserves, is a provisional relevance screen: 30 or 300 USDT/year.
   This is a research allocation rule, not a user investment preference or
   a universal profitability floor. Cash benchmarks of 0%, 3%, 5% remain separate.
   Small gains and idle capital must be visible. A test shorter than a year
   cannot claim its annualized rate is a forecast.
3. Execution/risk feasibility. Before advancement, report cash wallets, spot
   quantities/principal, collateral, net/gross notionals, funding/settlement,
   maintenance and stress paths, lot limits, bid/ask/latency and complete cases.
   Provisional screens: absolute BTC and ETH beta estimates at most 0.10 with
   simultaneous 95% intervals contained in [-0.20,0.20]; realized net base delta
   at most 1% of NAV; modeled maximum drawdown at most 10%; no cash/margin
   deficit in specified down-50%/up-100% and basis/liquidity stresses. Dollar
   neutrality alone does not pass. Uncertainty or unavailable margin terms
   prevents advancement rather than establishing failure of the mechanism.
4. Untouched confirmation and prospective local shadow evidence. Selection,
   sample sizing, dependence-aware uncertainty, multiplicity and repeated-look
   procedure must be committed before collection/inspection. No candidate is
   currently selected, and no confirmation or paper start is admitted here.
   A positive lower simultaneous 95% net-cash confidence bound plus the frozen
   relevance/risk screens is necessary, never sufficient alone. No arbitrary
   universal Sharpe floor. Historical p-values are not recycled as confirmation.
