Directional buy-and-hold, cosmetic indicator sweeps and old LLM recollections
do not meet the neutral mechanism mandate. Equity/new-venue execution,
institutional-only financing, paid history and deferred settlements are outside
the feasible execution scope. Staking/lending compensation is considered under
carry/venue/on-chain: principal/counterparty/slashing/depeg and hedge costs need
explicit admission; advertised yield alone does not justify a strategy test.
