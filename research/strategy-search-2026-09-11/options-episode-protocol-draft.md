## Durability and phases

1. Full source/history/grant admission and an exclusive claim precede networking.
   A source/source-design hash check accompanies each collection invocation.
2. The offline journal grants no network authority. The caller resolves exact
   request recipes against the immutable selection, publishes a bounded batch
   of intents, then makes at most one attempt per source slot. No network retry
   after ambiguous interruption, even if no complete body was retained.
3. Raw receipts/prefixes and wall/monotonic clocks precede interpretation. At a
   controlled restart, verify the active claim/source and complete journal,
   classify interrupted intents unavailable and past untouched slots missed,
   and proceed only with future eligible slots. A complete/failed outer terminal
   prohibits restart. The persistent lock is never replaced.
4. Full journal verification occurs at invocation/recovery/seal boundaries.
   Locked append operations use a validated, incrementally updated inventory;
   failed publication invalidates the cache. This assumes cooperating writers
   obey the same lock; no claim of hostile filesystem confinement. Global byte
   and member limits reserve room for an immutable failure seal.
5. Seal source evidence once. The source seal is not outer completion. An
   independent source review must bind all members, selection, clocks, funding
   coverage and unavailable states before the financial stage. Bindings supply
   only hashes/availability; no changed policy or source selection.
6. Claim the financial stage exclusively and evaluate the frozen eight cases
   once. Retain every case on arithmetic/source unavailability. A crash after
   an analysis intent cannot trigger an automatic economic replay. Finally
   publish one complete or failed outer receipt, all source and output hashes,
   and the complete intended denominator. A completed receipt is not a pass.
