separate observation, retrieval and version metadata at the schema level.

## Frozen inspection and denominator

Use the seven absolute paths and fields in the frozen input specification.
No alternative paths, recursive workspace search, network, credentials, corpus
records, signals, article values, return joins or models. Preserve all original
stores. Reject symlinks in any path component and record unavailability.

Six directory source slots enumerate only direct four-digit-year children and
01.parquet through 12.parquet under them, with at most1,000 encountered entries
per source including excluded entries. One iterator-only overflow probe may
encounter entry1001 without stat/open/name retention; overflow is partial and
withholds both footer selections because the global lexicalfirst2 are unknown.
Select the first two lexical eligible
monthly paths before opening metadata; retain two subordinate footer slots per
source even when absent. No replacement after a failure. Only the bounded
Parquet footer/schema is read, never data pages. Cap each footer at1MiB. Do not
publish row-group statistics or arbitrary key-value metadata; emit only frozen
whitelist field presence/types, column count and hashes of unknown field names.
Unsupported footer parsing is unavailable, not a reason to read records.

The seventh source is the exact external full_articles.csv. Read at most the
first physical line, up to64KiB, unbuffered without read-ahead. No records after
the line are read. Require strict UTF-8/CSV syntax; multiline or headerless
semantics stay unavailable. Whitelist recognized names case-insensitively,
retain collisions as ambiguity and hash all unknown names. Never persist raw
header text or arbitrary names that could be a content row. A plausible ID/time
field is required to call it a header. Names such as date have no inferred
publication or first-observation semantics.

For inspected metadata regions retain byte offset/length/hash and before/after
file identity/size/mtime/ctime to detect concurrent changes, including same-size
in-place rewrites with restored mtime. Both timestamps are filesystem attributes,
not historical public availability. These before/after metadata checks are
best-effort change detection, not an atomic snapshot guarantee; filesystem-clock
resolution can hide very rapid changes. Source observation UTC is now,
not the original publication time. No full CSV hash, article text copy or false
claim of recoverable corpus backup. Local file mtime is not availability.

Seven source cells and13 subordinate schema slots (12 footers +1 header) remain
in outputs, including unavailable/partial cases. Inventory status, schema status
and historical availability are separate. A complete source cell means its
bounded schema inspection completed, not causal admission. Missing directories,
truncation, read failures, invalid headers and unsupported schema remain explicit.
Historical availability, entity links, revision authenticity, completeness and
financial utility remain unverified regardless of column presence.
