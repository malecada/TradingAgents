Monotonic transport clocks, durable raw retention, restart/duplicate handling, auxiliary Greek/funding-field presence inventories and some clock/skew diagnostics remain future collector responsibilities. The adapter verifies caller-supplied raw bytes and identities; it does not itself acquire or durably archive them. Time calibration assumes stable offset during the short batch and a server timestamp generated within the response. EAPI Greek event time remains unknown. These qualifications must remain explicit rather than describing the proposal as fully implemented.

The engine is a pure conditional unit-one accounting function. It does not independently select contracts, admit current rule/minimum-quantity metadata, establish Greek dimensional applicability, enforce venue margins or implement the future episode's date/length policy. Its caller must bind those inputs and preserve all eight cases on invalid input or `ArithmeticScopeError`.
