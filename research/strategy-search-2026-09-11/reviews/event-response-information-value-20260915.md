# Independent event-response information-value review

September 15, 2026. **One bounded design step is justified, conditional on making
the question a cost-aware receiver-time falsification screen. A collector that
only establishes message ordering is not justified.** No allowance, capture,
implementation or financial experiment is approved by this review.

The worthwhile unknown is narrowly practical: after a publicly received leader
trade batch and a fixed processing delay, does a prespecified follower/hedge
pair retain enough subsequent, jointly observed bid/ask price movement to cover
the complete two-leg round-trip hurdle? Even this is an optimistic quote-based
screen, not fill evidence. A negative supported result can stop this particular
latency/execution proposition cheaply. A positive result would justify examining
execution and independent evidence; it would not demonstrate a neutral net edge.

## Evidence and ancestry

The discovery, current source matrix, predictive-coverage review, continuation
coverage and MAP were read. The old LLG result directly covers BTC/ETH-to-alt
follow/fade at hourly/daily horizons and retains a cost failure. Its missing
funding and explicit hedge qualification does not restore a promising neutral
book. OFLOW and liquidity history also bear directly on the proposed mechanism;
PRX/ETHBTC work prevents presenting a familiar pair as untouched. The existing
three-symbol spot capture does not contain the needed aggressive leader events
or perpetual quotes. No admitted saved source can reconstruct this local joint
receipt-time question.

The new documented fields create a measurable possibility: futures aggTrade
batches, individual follower and hedge book quotes, local monotonic receipt
clocks and sizes. They do not create evidence of delay or affordability. The
100 ms aggregation, different stream routes, RPI differences and incomplete
bookTicker loss detection are substantive limits. An apparent lead may be
transport scheduling, a common shock or missing messages. Timestamp ordering
cannot establish economic causation, queue priority or an order fill.

The primary ancestry is the exhausted liquidity/execution mechanism, with
explicit LLG/OFLOW and statistical-pair history. A later source/markout episode
would require a documented information-value extension before observations;
calling it relative value, metadata or a source check must not hide its empirical
outcomes or move it into an unused budget. The user's authorization supports the
ordinary design and reviewed amendment route; the existing cap is not a new
user-permission gap. This review judges information value, not whether more
research is conceivable.

## Why the narrower screen has conditional information value

The old bar tests cannot determine whether a short price adjustment persists
past actual message receipt and ordinary processing latency. A fixed immediate
relative-price response could therefore fail or remain feasible for reasons
not measured by hourly signal accuracy. Its prospective source requirement is
specific, and a short two-instrument episode could bound engineering cost.

The objective raises the bar: $1,000 cannot finance an assumption of maker
rebates, privileged feeds or zero-cost frequent hedging. A smaller number of
liquid legs is preferable to a post-hoc alt basket. At $10,000 minimum-lot effects
may improve, but neither a larger account nor more symbols creates predictive
information. Every proposed actionable opportunity must include the opposite
hedge, entry and exit spreads, quantities and all four fee-bearing leg
transitions. No same-time midpoint response or favorable maximum is an adequate
primary result.

A liquid prespecified instance is **BTCUSDT aggTrade as leader, ETHUSDT perpetual
as follower and BTCUSDT perpetual as opposite hedge**, on Binance USD-M. These
identities are proposed for the design because they minimize instrument count,
use the user's available venue and avoid outcome-selected follower universes.
They do not assert a contractual convergence relation or optimal follower.
One-to-one dollar sizing reduces gross common-direction exposure at entry but
is not a beta estimate or proof of market neutrality. BTC/ETH alone may be too
efficient for the hypothesis; that is an informative possibility, not a reason
to substitute a slower observed alt after failure.

## Concrete next action: one written finite protocol

Prepare exactly one source/markout protocol and its resource estimate. Do not
build a general event platform or acquire data before independent design and
allowance review. The following is a concrete candidate, still unregistered:

1. **One fixed stream recipe and interval.** BTCUSDT aggTrade plus individual
   BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT bookTicker, with required current instrument/lot/fee assumptions
   identified before capture. One future 30-minute interval, chosen for completed
   preparation rather than observed activity; at most 360 nonoverlapping
   five-second decision bins. No reconnect/retry after a gap, no second session
   chosen because the first is quiet, and no instrument, trigger or delay sweep.
   The protocol must specify and retain no-event and disconnected bins.
2. **One trigger and direction.** In each fixed bin take the first eligible
   locally received BTC aggregate-trade batch. Buy-initiated direction proposes
   long ETH/short BTC; sell-initiated proposes the reverse. The documented maker
   flag must be translated explicitly and synthetically checked. Do not select
   the largest impulse after observing a bin, tune a size threshold or interpret
   aggregated volume as visible-book depletion. Preserve all event batches and
   selection reasons even when only one event is scored.
3. **One action and holding interval.** A proposed fixed decision delay is
   250 ms after receipt; a proposed exit is 1,000 ms after that action. The
   design must state these as engineering assumptions, not measured latency
   advantage. It must also freeze quote-age limits and source clock treatment
   before observation. If the intended host cannot meet processing/resource
   requirements in an offline timing preflight, defer the protocol; do not
   repair it by selecting a faster successful delay from market outcomes.
4. **Actual information boundary.** Retain raw bytes, local wall/monotonic
   arrival times and per-socket sequence, exchange E/T fields, batching IDs,
   duplicates/conflicts, socket/discontinuity/gap reasons and fixed cutoff.
   The as-of entry state may use only quotes received by the action time;
   the as-of exit state may use only quotes received by its fixed exit time.
   A later quote cannot be backdated because its exchange event time is early.
   A quote received before action is still only the latest observed quote, not
   proof it remained executable when an order would arrive. Do not infer global
   exchange serialization from local order or bookTicker IDs.
5. **Two capital cases, one sizing rule.** Freeze $1,000/$10,000 capital cases
   without optimizing allocations from observed returns. Bind lot rounding,
   hedge-notional mismatch, minimum notionals, quantity caps and displayed size
   on both legs. A conservative unlevered sizing proposal must be justified
   in the design, not chosen here from absent instrument data. Retain wallet/
   collateral assumptions and full-capital normalization. Never silently score
   fractional quantities that the instrument cannot trade.
6. **Cost-first outcome.** For each supported bin compute a signed, two-leg
   entry-to-exit quoted-price markout, crossing ask/bid in the appropriate
   direction at all four transactions. Subtract the prespecified applicable or
   explicitly conditional taker fee assumptions and processing/slippage stress.
   Report quoted price-move headroom and the required break-even cost separately.
   Funding and non-atomic hedge execution cannot silently be set to zero and
   promoted to a complete net cash book. Either admit their event/cost treatment
   or explicitly limit this stage to the conditional price/cost screen.
7. **A fixed descriptive comparison.** Include a predeclared direction-null
   using the identical bins, quote states, lots, costs and support denominator,
   with independently fixed signs/seed. Preserve an unhedged response diagnostic
   to reveal how much apparent response is removed by the hedge; do not choose
   hedge ratios or signs after looking. These controls address direction/common
   movement descriptively. One short interval and correlated receipts cannot
   supply causal identification or expected-profit confidence from an IID test.
8. **Complete stopping and resource contract.** Before capture set explicit
   byte/message/CPU/memory/time caps, source-failure behavior, event and bin
   denominators, minimum joint coverage and the fixed primary cost screen.
   Count all 360 intended bins, eligible triggers, supported quotes/quantities,
   no-event, missing/stale/gap states and retained partial final bins. Quote
   failures must not disappear through complete-case selection. A supported
   cost-screen failure closes this precise instance. Inadequate source coverage
   yields unavailable, not an economic failure; it also does not automatically
   authorize another collection. Unexpected positive observations cannot
   authorize selecting another trigger, horizon or favorable subset.

The exact fee/capital/quote-age/coverage/resource numbers and primary threshold
remain deliberately unresolved **design inputs**. They must be filled using the
already documented source constraints and realistic user-capital execution,
then independently reviewed before any requests. This is a single bounded
protocol task, not permission to keep drafting or collecting until an appealing
result appears. A single lucky markout or “some positive opportunities” cannot
be its advancement gate; the design must use the whole supported bin population,
report availability selection, cost stress and the fixed comparison together.

## Rejection and advancement boundary

Reject the proposed acquisition if its final protocol only measures that a
quote arrives after a trade, uses exchange times as local availability, assumes
maker fills, omits the hedge or round-trip costs, selects follower/thresholds
from observed response, or cannot price the $1,000/$10,000 lot constraints.
Those outcomes would add little to existing cost-erased predictive evidence.
Likewise defer if the required source recipe cannot preserve usable common
clocks and bounded failure evidence on the intended host. These are concrete
missing capabilities, not a rejection of every predictive mechanism.

If the finite design survives those checks, a separate explicit liquidity-family
extension may be substantively justified for **one** prospective source/conditional
markout episode. That decision must bind its exact protocol, cumulative history,
source resources and untouched future interval before acquisition. No allowance
is granted here. The short source screen is suitable for a narrow falsification
or feasibility result, not annualized profit, low-beta validation or a statement
that a one-second quoted opportunity can actually be captured.

A positive conditional screen would leave actual fees/access, fills, adverse
selection, order/hedge latency, funding/collateral, residual BTC/ETH exposure,
frequency and independent net validation unresolved. It would not automatically
justify a longer sample or another parameter configuration. A negative one
would reject this precise entry/exit/cost hypothesis and update the map, without
claiming that all event-driven or predictive crypto research is exhausted.

No new external documentation, market body, socket, empirical output, code or
ledger was accessed for this review. The options experiment is outside this
protocol and remains governed by its frozen source/observation rules.

The proposed BTC-to-ETH identity, first-batch trigger and opposite dollar hedge
are a reviewable starting design, not an admitted causal specification. Defer
capture if the completed design cannot explain why this particular received
batch should predict incremental ETH-versus-BTC adjustment at its fixed horizon,
or if the hedge mainly subtracts the leader's own transient response and makes
the outcome non-comparable with the intended follower hypothesis. A merely
convenient arbitrary trigger/hedge is insufficient. Report any choice that is
only a conservative execution convention separately from the behavioral
hypothesis. No empirical beta fitting or favorable hedge search can repair that
rationale during this design task. Stop after one complete protocol and one
independent admission verdict; do not expand into a generic model/collector
platform. Frozen options and historical research runtimes remain unchanged.
