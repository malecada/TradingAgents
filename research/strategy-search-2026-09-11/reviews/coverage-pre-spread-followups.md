### 2. One bounded prospective SELL-premium design adjudication, not another ticket calculation

This is the most concrete remaining low-cost *design* task. Decide whether an
actual Binance short-option/hedge episode can be observed and accounted for with
the public sources, a fixed policy and meaningful limits. It is not yet a
justified empirical launch. An explicit design can either expose an affordable
informative test or identify the exact missing requirement; repeating the phrase
“prospective data needed” without that adjudication would leave a planning gap.

The distinction from inherited research is narrow. THESIS_FINDINGS §71, line5856,
records `predlab_rviv_p0`: HAR-30 versus an expanding point-in-time-debiased
Deribit DVOL predictor over June2022–March2025, with daily-simple-return RV and
QLIKE/DM-HAC30 evaluation. Its registered forecast comparison failed; descriptive
premium evidence also weakened the static-harvest story. All twelve ledger rows
and exposed observations remain. The old text's broad “static route dead” claim
does not amount to a literal Binance executable short-option cash book, but its
negative evidence must count against spending resources on one. No renamed HAR,
EWMA, realized-vol threshold, DVOL debiasing or favorable subperiod is a new lead.

The distinct, falsifiable question would concern actual short-option cash plus
an explicitly dynamic hedge: does the offered convexity/tail compensation cover
the realized hedge path, funding, spreads and fees on all prefunded capital?
No cheap buyer ticket supplies evidence for that inequality. A long-volatility
policy reverses the premise and requires independent motivation; it is not the
default follow-up. Nor is a static maker-mid or model-mark return a substitute
for the proposed selling book.

The minimum useful design memo should make the following concrete in one pass:

- One fixed representative contract rule and SELL orientation, minimum lots,
  entry/exit/expiry treatment, economic reason for the exposure, and a zero-edge
  explanation. Avoid a selected richest premium or post-loss side flip.
- A documented model-delta dimensional convention, fixed rehedging schedule and
  rounding/residual rule, with an explicit account of hedge transaction costs.
  A hedge schedule cannot be optimized after observing the path.
- Simultaneously supportable option bid/ask-size and hedge price observations,
  actual signed funding event marks, contract/settlement semantics, and a policy
  for missing or stale observations. Model marks can value but cannot fill.
  Known public endpoints do not demonstrate future completeness or queue fills.
- Conditional seller opening and position margin separately, prefunded wallets,
  no automatic options/futures netting, jump/gamma/tail losses and liquidation
  limitations. Public sell-to-open formulas exist; actual access/rules remain
  unknown. They belong in a motivated book, not another stand-alone capital fit.
- A finite observation duration, frequency, request/storage/CPU budget and
  coverage threshold adequate for the declared **episode feasibility** claim.
  A single expiry cannot validate expected profit; overlapping observations and
  BTC/ETH are not independent repetitions. Statistical confirmation needs its
  own untouched opportunity count and power justification, not a guessed number
  of calendar months or recycling HAR/DVOL observations.

An affordable short source rehearsal would only be justified if it settles a
specific technical uncertainty required by that fixed policy, such as whether
necessary option/hedge timestamps and fields can coexist in bounded capture.
Otherwise it duplicates the admitted public snapshot. A future multiweek path

If no defensible duration/coverage/risk design can be written within current
constraints, defer the selling book on those exact missing terms. Do not launch

This adjudication has value because it decides between an actual path test and
