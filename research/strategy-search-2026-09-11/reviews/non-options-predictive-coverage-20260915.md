## One genuinely unresolved source question

**Can the available public event schema and retained provenance establish that a leader BTC/ETH aggressive-trade or quote event was locally observable before a follower quote remained actionable, while an explicit simultaneous market/leader hedge can be priced?** The key distinction is event receipt order and surviving executable opportunity, not selecting a new candle horizon. The plausible mechanism is heterogeneous inventory adjustment or transmission of price discovery; it is a hypothesis, not an identified causal treatment. A common market shock, timestamp batching or asynchronous quote delivery can generate apparent lead/lag without tradable delay.

The first useful deliverable is a finite source-admission matrix, not a regression or backtest. It must identify exact exchange event/transaction clocks versus local request/receipt clocks, sequence and gap semantics, independently timestamped leader and follower observations, executable bid/ask and sizes on both follower and hedge, instrument identity/lot/minimum/collateral constraints, fee applicability and realized funding/settlement ownership. It must answer whether the archived source actually contains these fields and contemporaneous witnesses; a provider event timestamp or recent retrieval of an old trade is not historical local availability. No arrival order should be inferred from unsynchronized candle closes or file row order.

No suitably admitted synchronized leader/follower receipt-time book archive was established in the inspected catalogue. Hourly/daily kline manifests and the BTC/ETH five-minute store establish possible bar inputs, not this event-source property. The existing triangle stream answered its own spot conversion question and carries a frozen-cutoff defect; it is not automatically a fresh perpetual leader-response sample. If the required archive does not exist, a prospective finite collection would need a new fully registered source episode, exact pair/trigger/null definitions and budget approval. This audit requests no collection.

For the capital objective, a later test must price both sides of the hedge at the first supported post-observation action time, carry signed quantities, all costs and missing opportunities, and measure residual BTC/ETH exposure rather than infer neutrality from demeaning returns. At approximately $1,000 minimum lots and two-legged turnover can consume the putative delay; $10,000 changes sizing constraints, not evidence of an edge. No latency advantage or maker fill is assumed. A source pass can establish measurability only; predictive identification and economic significance remain separate later gates.
