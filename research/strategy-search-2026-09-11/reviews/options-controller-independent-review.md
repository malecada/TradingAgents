The following are concrete rollout blockers, intentionally left outside this finite prototype review:

1. **Independent new-protocol verification:** `verify_episode` calls the same `_admit` implementation as the controller (lines 351 onward). It is a self-check of grant, future-window and control bindings, not an independent reconstruction of the new grant and episode. The independent verifier and exact target certificate/gate review remain absent.
2. **Remote authority and quiescence:** `_source_binding`, `_stop_ack` and `_quiescent` (lines 245–261 and 311–312) validate hash-bound declarations or lease time. They do not establish that a remote process actually stopped, obeyed its lease or produced a semantically admitted source journal. The portable worker assignment, lease enforcement, authenticated return/stop evidence and source-admission adapter must establish those facts before using these transitions.
3. **Complete resource and output contract:** `write_output` (lines 321–325) has no whole-episode output/staging byte cap. The complete calendar, journal plus duplicate prefix/final storage, analysis assembly and publication resources are not integrated or proven under the exact final execution path. Local component measurements do not admit multiweek worker operation.
4. **Concrete prospective execution:** no final grant, actual window, resolved request assignment, scheduler or integrated semantic adapter exists. The deterministic selection, missed-slot rules, stop behavior and one post-window eight-case analysis must be bound before an actual claim or deployment. No current test establishes account eligibility, fills, expected returns, economic performance or cryptographic proof of a remote stop.
