# Information-value review: one dated measurement amendment

**Decision: yes to a bounded successor-runtime amendment implementation and
independent synthetic review; no financial execution is admitted yet.** One
additional, exactly named dated child has sufficient marginal information value
to justify that limited engineering. This review is a decision to prepare the
route, not approval of an unimplemented certificate or an automatic cap increase.

## Evidence for the decision

The original registered 56-day episode has admitted, retained inputs and an
independently checked fixed-quantity cash engine, but no completed economic
verdict. The first attempted book computed objects in RAM before its statistical
import failed under a virtual-address-space limit. The second attempt failed
during source verification before financial evaluation. Both terminal receipts
have no economic output files. It would be incorrect to say neither attempt
computed anything, that the first observation is fresh, or that failed harnesses
demonstrate poor economics. No proposed change depends on a favorable result.

The earlier independent recommendation was to defer while options admission and
the monitor repair progressed. Those conditions have materially advanced:
options metadata and one lexical archive probe completed, the triangle source
and static calculation completed, and guard v2 passed the combined real-statistics,
disposable lifecycle and short-lived Git-helper preflight. Subsequent guarded
captures and calculation also completed. This supports reduced engineering risk;
it is not a guarantee that the next dated invocation succeeds.

Completing the unchanged dated episode would distinguish its conditional
cash/fee/basis behavior from the already evaluated perpetual-funding mechanism.
The economic answer remains unknown. A negative result would narrow this fixed
configuration; a positive result would motivate further feasibility questions
without validating expected returns, actual margin or present-day execution.
Neither outcome can erase the older negative dated scenarios or spent history.

The incremental acquisition cost is zero: no new market source, sample, parameter
search, paid resource or account capability is required. Existing synthetic
accounting evidence can be retained. This is a stronger reason to complete a
specific unanswered measurement than to collect more same-format option days
without semantics or repeatedly refresh the already negative fee-paying triangle
snapshot. It is not a claim that dated basis is more likely profitable than an
untested distinct mechanism; no probability of success or expected value estimate
is supported by the evidence.

## Opportunity cost and bounded scope

Meaningful alternatives remain. Bitrue contract/funding comparability and an
economically distinct relative-value admission have unresolved dependencies.
The program is not exhausted. Their ordinary public-source progress should
continue while this narrow engineering is reviewed; no blanket suspension of
other families is warranted.

The implementation must be a bounded policy exception over the preserved
lifecycle, with only the code needed to validate and consume one named allowance.
Do not turn this into a general budget-reset feature or a broad runtime redesign.
If preserving the existing admission, concurrency, lineage and byte-retention
checks requires substantially broader redesign, or targeted tests/review cannot
establish equivalent protection, leave dated work deferred and prioritize the
distinct source-admission questions. Sunk preparation effort alone is not a
reason to continue expanding the harness.

## Conditions the eventual implementation must satisfy

The proposal is sound only if all of these properties are reviewable before a
new claim:

- Preserve every original runtime file, gate/family object, raw input, claim and
  terminal receipt. Historical cap four remains visibly four; record a separate
  allowance of exactly one, yielding at most five cumulative administrative
  attempts for this mechanism, not a reset to a new baseline.
- Bind an explicit certificate to one target experiment ID and its failed parent,
  the complete prior claim/terminal inventory and hashes, committed amendment
  rationale, independent final implementation review and exact registration.
  A renamed family, missing claim, different target or changed history cannot
  receive the allowance. This information-value review alone is not a substitute
  for the later implementation review.
- Freeze identical economic source/helper hashes, input bytes and identities,
  asset/capital/cost cases, date cut, accounting/statistics, output interpretation
  and no-graduation rules. Changed runtime/guard/entry-point bytes must be separately
  declared; they cannot conceal an economic configuration change.
- Preserve the original exposed samples and known historical attempts. Claim
  consumption, including failures or partial runs, remains irreversible. The
  exclusive start/lock must prevent simultaneous starts or replay; another failure
  spends this one allowance and returns the question to deferral.
- Keep ordinary admission closed at the old cap. Only the exact certified target
  may use the addition. Validate the exception consistently at start, input read,
  output write, finish and independent verification, including source/registration
  stability throughout the run. Reject altered inputs, economic sources/config,
  certificate, review, target, missing inventory and reused allowance.
- Use invented tests for both normal and exceptional paths, concurrency/reuse,
  full lifecycle and immutable evidence. Run a nonsingular full dated synthetic
  pipeline under the final successor and guard v2 before final review. Commit,
  push and verify the complete freeze before any newly admitted financial claim.

These are required acceptance properties, not a claim that the proposed successor
already implements them. Their implementation and adversarial tests remain a
separate bounded review task.

## Status and limitations

No new empirical calculation, market input inspection, code/gate amendment,
certificate or research claim was created by this review. Only this document
was written. Dated financial work remains deferred until the explicit route,
exact child and final source are implemented, independently reviewed and frozen.
If admitted later, one completed result still requires independent raw-input
cash/statistics reconstruction and a qualified economic decision.

The return sign, actual liquidation safety, current applicability, expected
profitability and superiority to other families remain unknown. Zero strategies
are validated. No automatic second extension or general future recovery policy
follows from this narrowly justified engineering decision.
