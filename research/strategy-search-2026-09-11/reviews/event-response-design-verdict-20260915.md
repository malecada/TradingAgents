# Independent event-response design verdict

September 15, 2026. **DEFER capture, implementation and allowance for the current draft.** The finite design step is complete. The draft carefully disclaims fills, causation and a complete financial book, but does not yet satisfy the independent information-value conditions. The blockers concern the meaning of the prediction and the decision rule, not merely missing collector code. No empirical data, source requests, financial engine, gate or historical file was changed; only this verdict was written.

## Material blockers

1. **The proposed inherited sign does not identify this hypothesis.** The old result concerned a BTC hourly bar return predicting an alt-index next-bar return. The draft replaces both the explanatory variable and target with BTC buyer/seller aggregate flow and an ETH-versus-BTC one-second quote response. A negative bar-return slope does not establish a negative aggressive-flow response, ETH specifically, or that horizon. Calling the direction inherited overstates what the prior test identifies. The review itself proposed the opposite follow direction; neither proposed sign is presently established by the cited mechanism. Choosing one because it is an existing negative coefficient is not a causal bridge. A written behavioral rationale with a clear falsifiable follower-versus-leader target must precede a source episode; no comparison of both signs on new outcomes is admitted.

2. **The hedge can become the signal being measured.** For buyer-initiated flow, the draft shorts ETH and buys BTC. An invented case with ETH unchanged and BTC rising after entry makes the gross spread positive entirely through the BTC leg, without delayed ETH adjustment. Conversely, rising ETH with flat BTC makes this negative. The opposite sign has the mirror issue. Equal-dollar sizing reduces instantaneous directional notional but does not isolate follower adjustment or beta neutrality; contemporaneous BTC movement and ETH movement have distinct meanings. An unhedged ETH and separate BTC-leg diagnostic plus a same-state fixed direction comparison would expose this decomposition, but diagnostics alone cannot supply the missing economic rationale. Do not fix it by choosing a favorable hedge ratio, estimating a new beta, removing the hedge, or renaming a BTC continuation result an ETH lag.

3. **Any positive maximum is an inadequate advancement gate.** The draft advances if any supported event/case has positive after-fee quote difference. The information-value review explicitly rejects a single lucky markout or some positive opportunities as the primary result. Thousands of overlapping events and four correlated cost/capital cells can generate isolated positives through noise. Retaining the denominator without using it in the decision does not solve this. A zero-positive result can falsify a narrow necessary condition in that window; its complement cannot by itself justify a follow-up. A valid finite screen must predeclare a whole-population descriptive cost comparison, support/coverage requirements and fixed same-state controls, with no statistical significance claim. No numerical success threshold is invented by this reviewer.

4. **Changed sampling and duration lack a demonstrated information tradeoff.** Ten minutes/all unique events replaces the review's proposed thirty minutes/at most 360 nonoverlapping five-second bins. Neither window length is independently validated. More overlapping events are not more independent information; they can repeatedly select the same transient quote and increase apparent opportunities. The proposed caps also lack an event/message-count bound despite every event requiring multiple case/witness records. A single bounded nonoverlapping sampling recipe with retained no-event bins is a defensible engineering choice, but should not be silently substituted now or treated as proof of adequate power. A predeclared whole-population target, realistic message/output bound and minimum source support must explain the chosen design. A quiet or failed episode must not authorize a replacement sample.

5. **Required source protocol is still incomplete.** Venue-event freshness, event/receipt compatibility, aggregation semantics, unknown feed loss and gap treatment are named remaining requirements rather than frozen tests. A recently received quote may itself be old. A common local clock on two connections does not prove exchange ordering, and bookTicker IDs do not establish contiguous coverage. The proposal must state exactly which event/time witnesses can be admitted, how clock uncertainty affects entry/exit ownership, when a gap contaminates a window, whether all intended denominators become unavailable after a source failure, and what absence of loss detection prevents it from claiming. Exact rules/fees/funding-boundary bindings and terminal sealing/resource headroom are also unresolved. A no-reconnect rule bounds behavior but does not establish source completeness.

## What is acceptable and what is not required

The draft appropriately retains four fixed capital/fee scenario cells, bid/ask crossings on both legs, quantity rounding, explicit fees, no double spread charge, all unavailable events, no summation of overlapping counterfactual returns and no automatic funding-zero assumption. These are useful invariants. The 250 ms action and one-second hold can be labeled engineering conventions, but are not evidence that the intended information persists at those horizons. Half-capital gross sizing is an explicitly conditional quote scenario, not proof of collateral affordability. Active options and old source packages are correctly excluded from mutation.

A calibrated randomization p-value is not necessary for a genuinely descriptive feasibility screen. Its absence is therefore not a demand to build an inference platform. However, the draft also omits the fixed descriptive direction comparison and per-leg diagnostic required by the information-value review. A no-p-value disclaimer cannot excuse an any-positive advancement rule. A later scientific predictive claim would need independent inference and untouched confirmation; that is beyond this step.

## Finite correction boundary and disposition

The finite written corrections are: identify the exact economically motivated response and sign without claiming the old bar result establishes it; show what the hedge-adjusted target measures; replace any-positive advancement with a predeclared whole-population descriptive rule and fixed same-state controls; select one bounded event/bin denominator and support requirement; and finish the exact source-clock/gap/resource contract. These are admission prerequisites, not approval to keep designing alternatives until one looks attractive. The current materials do not establish the first two requirements, so implementation is not justified merely by the availability of the streams.

Retain the draft and this deferral as the result of the one approved design step. No new collector, liquidity-family extension, empirical claim, seventh options allowance, parameter sweep or further market observation follows. Reconsider only if a specific additional non-outcome mechanism/source argument resolves the named target and information-value blockers under a separately bounded decision. This rejects the present acquisition design, not all cross-asset prediction or future economically justified relative-value research.
