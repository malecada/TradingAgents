## One proposed policy, with rationale rather than return-selected parameters

**Mechanism:** sell convexity demanded by other participants, hedge first-order
underlying exposure, and bear the residual jump/gamma/volatility risk. A matched
call and put at one near-at-the-money strike avoids picking a directional option
side. This is an ordinary variance-risk-bearing proxy, not a pure variance swap;
its strike/time weighting and tails differ from an index. No HAR/DVOL entry
forecast, volatility threshold, skew selection or premium ranking is used.

- BTC and ETH are fixed alternative books, never a pooled two-asset capital
  claim. Compare1,000 and10,000USDT and both frozen fee cases: eight primary
  books, each containing two short options and its changing perpetual hedge.
- At the first UTC hourly observation after a separately committed launch,
  choose expiry nearest30days in the inherited7–45day band, earlier expiry
  as tie. Choose the shared CALL/PUT strike closest to the contemporaneous index,
  lower strike then lexical identity as tie. Both must have admitted unit1,
  consistent lots and USDT settlement metadata. Missing shared candidates stay
  unavailable; do not pick the richest premium or replace an illiquid contract.
- Sell the smallest common valid CALL/PUT quantity once. Do not scale up with
  spare capital or resize to pass relevance. This deliberately asks whether
  minimum tradable inventory permits an interpretable small-account book.
  It cannot optimize the strategy's capacity or demonstrate the best return/C.
- Buy back both options at the first hourly decision at or after expiry minus
  24hours; simultaneously close the hedge. This avoids relying on newly inferred
  settlement semantics and avoids the last-day gamma concentration. The one-day
  buffer is an operational rule, not a proven best exit; it is frozen for both
  assets. Do not hold through expiry after a failed close. Missing terminal
  executable observations make final cash unavailable, not zero or mark-filled.
- Rehedge at each UTC hourly decision, using only the just-received model deltas
  for the fixed two options. Under the declared unit1 model, offset their sum
  in the conventional same-underlying USDT perpetual. Round to the nearest
  admissible futures lot, tie toward zero; do not trade if no valid nonzero
  order satisfies minimum rules. Retain rounding delta and every no-trade reason.
  Hourly control is a fixed operational compromise, not optimized gamma timing.
- Model delta is conditional, never true realized beta. The dimensional
  convention must be stated and synthetically tested before any actual values
  are evaluated. A direction/quantity value is not calculated in this report.

The policy has unbounded theoretical short-call loss and short-gamma jump risk;
a finite reserve is not a defined-loss payoff. Do not falsely describe a
delta hedge or stop instruction as a guaranteed loss cap. A defined-risk vertical
or iron structure would add purchased options and change the exposure/premium,
requiring its own motivation rather than a silent rescue. No actual selling
is proposed before tail/account feasibility is established.

## Accounting, costs and risk scope

Allocate40%C to the options wallet,50%C to the perpetual wallet and10%C idle,
with no automatic transfer or cross-product offset. Keep option-sale premium
cash together with its marked liability; receipt of premium is not immediate
profit or justification for more inventory. Apply public order-initial and
position-initial requirements separately under an explicitly empty initial
account, while retaining actual applicability as unknown. A failed resource
condition is reported; do not add leverage, collateral or another quantity.

Use option SELL bid for entry and BUY ask for exit, quantities limited by
displayed size. Preserve both option transaction fee assumptions0.00024 and
0.00030 with the documented premium-based cap; neither is the user's fee.
Use the inherited futures0.0005 fee and0.0002 adverse slippage, doubled in the
stress case. Couple the0.00024option-fee case with base futures costs and the
0.00030case with doubled futures costs. Base options use the best bid/ask only
when its displayed size covers the whole required quantity; otherwise the
decision is unavailable/no trade under the frozen missing-decision rule.
Stress worsens each option execution by one admitted price tick (sell lower,
buy higher), without adding synthetic depth. A nonpositive stressed sell price
is unavailable. The extra tick is a fixed execution sensitivity, not a claim
about actual slippage. All hedge turnover,
entry/exit fees and actual signed funding event marks belong in cash. Futures
notional is not sale proceeds. No fee is subtracted twice or premium counted
both as revenue and mark appreciation.

Funding events must be joined to hedge inventory held immediately before the
event under a committed clock convention; an hourly hedge and funding boundary
collision cannot be resolved retrospectively in the profitable direction.
Valuation, traded cash and model signals are separate streams. Missing mark
valuation leaves risk metrics unavailable even if terminal cash can reconcile.
Borrowing, fee assets, commission eligibility, actual margin tiers, funding
exceptions and liquidation fills remain admitted inputs or explicit unknowns.

Report minimum-wallet balances, all commissions/funding/hedge cash, terminal
cash/C, the full-capital3% descriptive relevance comparison and separate0/3/5%
cash benchmarks. Preserve quote age/skew, skipped hedges and lot residuals.
Fixed down50%/up100% jumps before the next hedge, doubled costs, loss of one
hedge opportunity and separate-wallet stress expose risks that continuous-delta
algebra hides. None supplies a probabilistic tail bound. A reserve deficit or
unknown intraday margin prevents advancement. A market-neutral label cannot
replace measured BTC/ETH exposure with uncertainty.

**Null:** premiums do not compensate the actual hedge path, funding and costs;
any apparent profit reflects short-tail exposure, crypto beta or an execution
proxy. Fixed same-inventory zero-funding and frictionless decompositions can
isolate costs but do not establish the null's probability. One episode has no
credible expected-profit confidence or power. Both favorable and adverse
episodes remain development evidence; a positive episode cannot graduate.

## Smallest informative duration and engineering burden

The smallest economic study of this fixed policy is **one complete selected
holding interval**, approximately6–44days under the7–45day entry band and
24hour pre-expiry close. Duration is determined by the frozen selection; no
eight-minute sample captures earned premium versus the future hedge path.
One interval answers conditional episode accounting/cost/risk feasibility only.
It cannot estimate expected selling premium or repeated annual returns. There
is no defensible universal minimum number of future expiries for confirmation
without dependence/tail-power assumptions; inventing “three months is enough”
would overstate evidence. Further episodes need their own justified staged plan.

An indicative upper bound at44days is1,056hourly decisions. A straightforward
batch has16public requests (EAPI time, two indices, four depths, four marks;
FAPI time, two depths, two current mark observations), plus two final-funding
requests per day and two initial metadata requests. This is about17,000requests,
not a tiny source probe, but it needs no paid data or large model. With an8KiB
cap per routine response and5MiB per initial metadata response, raw retention
is about144MiB; a512MiB total output ceiling is plausible only with single-copy
raw storage and measured lifecycle encoding. Oversize bodies retain bounded
prefixes as unavailable; do not raise caps after seeing responses. These are
resource estimates, not observed payload sizes or approved limits.

Two CPU/512MiB per short acquisition/replay process and a bounded per-batch
deadline are plausible. The essential additional resource is reliable collection
across days, with clocks, durable state and explicit missing-slot treatment.
Actual source adapters, body caps, request weights and full-lifecycle size must
be tested synthetically before any capture grant. No job, unattended process,
recurring automation or future quote revisit is created by this document.

## Immediate-action adjudication

There is **no new high-value eight-minute source experiment** identified here.
The current capture already admitted option metadata, call bid/ask/size and
model-mark response shapes; the prospective put uses the same documented depth
schema. Another current capital or generic quote-synchronization screen would
not resolve the time-path null. A short bounded rehearsal could verify new
collector engineering using invented responses without consuming market data.
Only a particular unresolved live-source behavior that synthetic tests and the
retained evidence cannot settle could justify a separately frozen short probe.

The meaningful next empirical option action would be a **single full-interval
prospective episode**, after this policy's price-stress/clock/model-rule
conventions and capture implementation are concretely reviewed. It is feasible
in public-source principle and modest in compute/storage, but it cannot produce
its economic answer immediately. Account facts are required for practical
seller feasibility, not to pretend the public conditional episode is impossible.
The design is therefore a specific future-observation dependency, not an
evidence-based rejection of every option mechanism.

Whether to allocate that duration now should be decided after the fixed spread
diagnosis, weighing the inherited adverse HAR/DVOL/static-premium evidence and
the limited information from one episode. Do not launch it merely to avoid a
checkpoint; do not call it infeasible merely because the initial three slots
are spent. Any approval needs an explicit cumulative options extension and
