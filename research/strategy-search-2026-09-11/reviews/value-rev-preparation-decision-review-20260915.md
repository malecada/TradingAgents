## Decisions that still need a distinct policy choice

The pending C2 sign/return definition, A's missingness/shift contract, rank ties
and leg rounding, first/last scored bar and terminal liquidation conventions
are not facts that a provider request can settle. They can be prepared and
synthetically reviewed before the grid, but this review does not force them into
one unnoticed implementation default. Each affects comparisons or cost and
needs its own explicit rationale tied to the inherited engine and charter.

Minimum constraints are already clear: score every registered dev date, place
any accounting anchor outside the scored interval, retain the first day's
capital charge, apply identical clocks and endpoints to value/C1/C2/placebos,
and distinguish marked continuation from liquidation with closing costs.
Dollar neutrality at rebalance does not imply continuous low crypto-price
exposure. A final accounting adapter must retain signed contracts, simple PnL,
fees and the single full-NAV risk-free charge. No legacy main or partial grid
should run while these contracts remain pending.
