## Incremental information and proportionality

This is not a new independent alpha hypothesis. At matched quantities and
timestamps, its gross price/funding cash is algebraically related to the two
already inspected spot-financed books. Its information value is a properly
aligned decomposition and capital/risk accounting for two separately funded
derivatives: whether the retained relative-price path plus short-perpetual
funding covers both legs' costs, and whether each wallet survives the declared
conditional path diagnostics. Subtracting earlier reported totals from different
windows or quantities would not answer that question. No absence of spot
principal, apparent leverage capacity or nominal delta cancellation constitutes
evidence of profit.

One fixed offline evaluation is more informative than another isolated options
entry-capital screen without a motivated volatility prediction, a triangle fee/
time threshold change, or a WBETH allocation change. It requires no new data
purchase, network acquisition, account capability or future wait. Value-revision
vintage, news provenance, semantically admitted options paths and Bitrue settled
events remain distinct dependencies. This comparison does not prove exhaustive
coverage of every possible strategy or promise that a seventh attempt succeeds.

Another separately versioned admission package has real review and maintenance
cost. It is proportionate here only as a bounded preservation mechanism for this
single specified book, using already tested lifecycle behavior and explicit
closed inventories. It must not become a general budget manager or an automatic
chain of further research. There is no justification to build new execution,
portfolio-margin, scheduler or search infrastructure for this attempt. If a
concrete preservation/resource defect arises, diagnose it and reassess the
remaining value explicitly; do not silently keep granting retries.

## Scientific boundaries required before results

Approve preparation of exactly eight primary cases: BTC/ETH, alternative full
capital 1,000/10,000 USDT and base/doubled transaction costs. Fix May 1 entry
through June 25 close, 2026, long dated and short perpetual, equal base quantity,
and the inherited common assumed lots. These are correlated exploratory cases
on an exposed episode, selected with knowledge of earlier research. They are
not eight independent trials or a fresh sample. The chosen direction cannot be
reversed or the date/expiry/quantity rule adjusted in response to this outcome.

The proposed sizing inequality is admissible as a deliberately conservative
notional ceiling: largest common-step q satisfying
q*[max(dated entry ask, perpetual entry bid)+both per-unit entry fees] <= 0.40C.
Allocate 0.40C dated reserve, 0.50C perpetual reserve and 0.10C idle. Each asset
uses its own complete C; no portfolio pools the alternatives without a new
contract. Fees reduce their respective wallets; neither future notional is
cash credited or spot principal debited. Require exact terminal release of both
reserves, signed price cashflows, funding and all execution costs. Separately
retain final pre-exit valuation and zero-holdings post-exit cash.

Freeze inherited futures fee 0.0005 and adverse slippage 0.0002 per side, doubled
only for the transaction-cost scenario. Historical applicability remains assumed.
Funding rates are not doubled; the start-plus-five-second ownership exclusion,
signed negative payments and captured event identities remain explicit. All
input receipt/checksum/contract/time admissions must be reconstructed before
book use, including the newly acquired marks. No zero-filling or interpolation
may repair missing funding, trade bars or marks. Require positive activity at
the trade endpoints, not in ignored mark-bar fields.

Retain exactly two same-q diagnostic scalars inside each case: identical costs
with zero funding and raw trade-price cash plus actual funding with transaction
costs removed. These are 16 declared scalar diagnostics, not 16 additional daily
books or selectable candidates. Preserve the 72 specified hypothetical stress
states across the eight cases, including every deficit. They do not create 72
observed episodes. All source, primary-case, scalar and stress denominators must
be explicit in the charter and output schema.

Separate-wallet diagnostics must not offset one wallet's deficit with the
other's gain. The one-percent maintenance buffer and daily low/high stress are
conditional assumptions, not recovered exchange rules or actual liquidation
proof. Hypothetical stress cash must distinguish maintenance requirements from
fees; maintenance is not deducted a second time as a closing cash expense.
The 40/50/10 sizing and reserve split cannot be relaxed to manufacture survival.

Report terminal signed cash and full-capital relevance separately from the
0/3/5-percent cash benchmarks. The log-return sum is an explicitly invalid
arithmetic-PnL shadow; neither log sums nor daily simple-return sums replace
terminal compounded cash. Nonpositive NAV must preserve signed cash evidence
while making incompatible return/log/exposure diagnostics unavailable.

Use the proposed joint BTC/ETH HAC-lag-7 regression only as descriptive exposure
evidence. Its 97.5-percent coefficient intervals yield the stated Bonferroni
nominal within-book comparison, not guaranteed finite-sample coverage or
family-wide confirmation across eight cases. Missing expected-profit confidence
and power remain unavailable for one convergence episode per asset; favorable
point relevance or estimated beta cannot remedy this. Nominal equal-q linear
base delta is a modeled identity, not empirical beta, an observed margin rule
or an executable low-risk result. No case can graduate on these diagnostics.
