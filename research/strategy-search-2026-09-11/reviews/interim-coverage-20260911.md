## Ranked next distinct questions

1. **Do both fixed USDT/BTC/ETH spot conversion cycles clear necessary displayed
   spread, quantity and cost conditions at the two capital sizes?** The source
   review `neutral-alternatives-source-20260911.md` supplies a finite six-request
   design, with both directions and all capital/cost cells retained. This tests
   simultaneous cross-rate consistency, a different mechanism from funding,
   forecast momentum and passive OFLOW selection. Minimum dependencies are
   actual three-symbol identities/filters and the frozen public observations;
   no fitting, borrow or new venue is needed. It is high-information and cheap,
   but asynchronous snapshots only yield conditional quote scenarios. A
   negative snapshot cannot reject triangles timelessly, and a positive one
   cannot establish simultaneous fills or steady income.

2. **Can the already qualified volume-forecast information be linked to an
   observable execution-cost dependency without creating a directional signal?**
   Findings §96 reports material saved volume-loss improvements while mean-return
   comparisons do not support an edge. The inexpensive first step is a source/
   timing join admission: what execution observations would actually be
   available after each saved forecast, and do those observations identify
   spread/depth/impact rather than a volume proxy alone? This is a question
   motivated by previously registered information, not a hypothesis invented
   from a new favorable outcome. It needs no refit at the admission stage.
   A later fixed execution-cost comparison would inherit forecast selection,
   OFLOW/liquidity ancestry and exposed windows. Cost reduction is not itself a
   standalone return mechanism; it can improve a separately justified neutral
   book or inform why liquidity provision remains blocked. Do not register a
   financial policy until that connection is explicit.

3. **Do Binance and Bitrue expose a genuinely comparable, accessible hedge pair
   under documented public contract and funding rules?** A finite source-only
   matrix can resolve units, settlement currencies, event conventions, margin
   wallets, quantity limits and free-history constraints before any differential
   quote capture. No pair is established in saved evidence. This retains venue
   segmentation as a real coverage gap without assuming one deposit can fund
   both legs, transfers are instantaneous, borrow is available or account
   eligibility follows from API access. If rules cannot be matched, preserve
   that precise source barrier and move on rather than infer no venue premium.

These ranks are research-allocation judgments, not predicted financial rankings.
Options metadata already in progress can proceed alongside them after its
