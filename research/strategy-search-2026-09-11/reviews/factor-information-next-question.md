### 1. Saved news observation provenance — cheap and actionable now

Freeze a metadata-only question: **Do the saved article stores retain separately
verifiable source observation, publisher timestamp, retrieval/version identity
and entity-link provenance sufficient to admit any causal attention input?**
The catalogue currently says the workspace `News_fulltext/` directory has no
admitted content inventory, publication-vintage audit or external backup proof.
Findings §63 describes two earlier Alpaca/GDELT stores, but narrative “PIT” and
article-count claims do not establish the exact bytes available at a decision.

Recommended first registration: inventory only the named corpus/collector
manifests and schema/provenance fields, no article text or trading outcomes;
cap at 1,000 directory entries and 32 deterministically chosen metadata records
per documented source. Enumerate before choosing records; retain missing paths,
unsupported formats, truncation and every intended slot. Inspect collector code
for whether publication time is copied, retrieval time is local, and full text
is fetched later than the cited event. Do not treat file mtime or a modern
snapshot hash as historical first availability. No new news retrieval or LLM
call is needed to answer this first question. Exact paths/fields must be frozen
from the bounded inventory before record inspection; no unrestricted corpus
scan or implicit content access is proposed.

An eventual distinct information prediction could concern **reversal of a
previously published issuer-specific claim**, with a later correction changing
the expected cashflow/supply information while the broad crypto market is
hedged. That differs from generic article sentiment or pool-quality ranking.
It is only a candidate mechanism, not a registered prediction here: versioned
claims, verified issuer mapping, reproducible correction labels, event timing,
controls and shortable instruments are missing. Do not invent labels or an
event calendar from favorable returns. Passing source provenance alone would
not revive failed C1 extraction or establish such a trading signal.
