# Preparation review — value_rev ranks, endpoints and nulls

September 15, 2026. The proposed rules are defensible as explicit prospective additions, subject to the exact qualifications below. They are not clauses already settled by the original charter, and this review does not adopt them, admit source data or run a financial experiment. The [frozen charter](../../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-value-rev-charter.md), [engine parity audit](value-rev-engine-parity-audit-20260915.md), [pre-outcome interpretation](value-rev-preoutcome-interpretation-20260915.md) and [control declaration review](value-rev-control-assembly-review-20260915.md) supply the scope. Only this report was written; no outcomes, market bodies, network requests or engine executions were used. Examples are invented.

## Recommended rank contract

Preserve the existing leg-size and short-first priority instead of introducing floor rounding. Direct inspection of `tradingagents/xsect/ls_common.py:47–64` confirms a minimum of five valid names, `max(1, round(n * breadth))`, descending-score/ascending-identifier shorts first, and ascending-score/ascending-identifier longs excluding selected shorts. There is no identified parity or accounting reason to change these economic choices to floor rounding or a single ordering with last-k shorts. Those earlier proposals were not adopted.

State breadth as exact rational `1/3` or `1/10` and compute `k = max(1, nearest_integer_ties_to_even(n / denominator))`. This explicitly preserves the intended existing nearest/ties-to-even rule without binary-fraction ambiguity. For example, n=8 tercile gives k=3 rather than floor's 2; n=5 decile gives k=1 after the minimum. Retain the five-name floor for every role. Require finite admitted scores, unique canonical instrument identities, k valid members in each leg and disjoint legs. Missing required members, insufficient n, an invalid scheduled date or an all-equal score vector makes the comparison unavailable, rather than silently skipping a rebalance and carrying previous contracts. The explicit unavailable behavior is the correction; changing the rounding is unnecessary. The all-equal guard must be stated explicitly rather than attributed to a guard inside `ls_weights`, which does not itself check it.

Define an orientation-aware economic sorting score E for which high means short and low means long: E is the supplied score for `low_is_long`, and its negation for `high_is_long`. This is a sorting convention, not permission to choose the unresolved C2 construction. Choose shorts first by `(-E, canonical identifier)`, then longs by `(E, canonical identifier)` excluding shorts. Preserve the identifier tie priority in each direction; do not reverse identifier order with the score direction. Require the exact k names on both sides, never a smaller remaining long leg. Apply this same policy to strategy/C1/C2 and both null families. Canonical identifiers and alias rejection must be fixed before outcomes; response order is not an identifier policy.

Partial score ties can create persistent incidental identifier exposure. Retain tie counts and the convention in metadata without selecting another tie rule after results. All-equal scores contain no ordering information and remain unavailable. The P1 median breadth floor does not prove every scheduled decision supports every book. Metric-cohort selection and C2 orientation/aggregation remain separate prerequisites.

## Recommended endpoint contract

Freeze UTC economic intervals and their exact valuation/execution boundary instants before source admission. The dev date labels January 1, 2021 through March 31, 2025 are inclusive: if those labels identify UTC days, the scored interval is `[2021-01-01T00:00Z, 2025-04-01T00:00Z]`, with daily increments attributed to the preceding day. This interpretation requires supported source timestamp semantics; it is not inferred from a file index. April 1 boundary valuation may be necessary to close March 31, but no April 1 economic interval or holdout signal/return may enter dev fitting or its nulls.

Use a valuation/accounting anchor outside the scored return vector, flat positions at the start boundary, and the first scored interval's full-NAV capital charge even while flat. A prior anchor is bookkeeping, not a pre-dev trading decision. Signals may use separately admitted pre-dev lookback history without opening a pre-dev position. Initial trade costs, all held-interval cashflows and final liquidation costs belong to the scored ledger exactly once. Define the charge convention and cashflow ordering in the common accounting contract; flat start does not excuse omitting the first day's charge.

A Monday-close decision executes at the **immediate registered next daily boundary**, supported by admitted executable/valuation data. “Next supported” must not search forward across missing dates. At a boundary shared by the previous close and next open, define which source observation is executable and demonstrate that signal receipt precedes action; equal date labels alone do not prove availability. Missing boundary support or held inputs makes the book unavailable. The endpoint close uses the exact registered final boundary, never a later available price. Do not enter or rebalance a new position at the terminal boundary and immediately liquidate it: terminal liquidation takes precedence and a decision whose action lies at or after termination is outside the trading-action schedule. Apply this schedule to observed and null books and both controls.

Final liquidation is a genuine addition to the old marked-continuation behavior. Source support must include the final execution valuation and costs, funding ownership around the boundary and any held settlement. Daily close prices alone do not prove realized event funding. The endpoint proposal does not resolve that separate blocker.

## Recommended B null

For each fixed metric decision cohort, permute only the eligible finite score values among that same cohort, then apply the identical deterministic rank, count, orientation and execution policy. Never permute NaNs from outside the cohort into it. Freeze the full eligible index before randomization, with no post-permutation intersection or shrinkage. Preserve the score multiset and scheduled n and k exactly. Ties may make multiple permutations produce the same portfolio; retain those duplicates. An all-equal row is unavailable under the common score rule, including for the observed book; it cannot be rescued by random jitter. Partial ties may produce identical portfolios across several draws, which remain retained rather than redrawn.

Freeze 500 draw identifiers and a reproducible random-generation specification before generating any draw. Specify the generator/version, master seed, domain separation by family/metric/draw, canonical symbol/date order and whether each date's permutation is independently sampled. Prefer one randomized score panel per metric/draw, shared across its two breadths; retain four scored cells and do not treat that sharing as independent evidence. A missing required input or failed book remains attached to its draw identity. B may preserve score counts while still encountering unavailable funding or endpoint support; source failure is never a zero-return draw.

## Recommended A null and limitations

The proposed A preserves the charter's per-symbol circular-shift operation: freeze the full scheduled dev decision index of length T and each symbol's full score/missingness vector. Draw one offset per symbol uniformly from integers 1 through T-1, independently under the fixed random specification. T < 2 is unavailable. Freeze shift direction, for example `shifted[s,t] = original[s,(t-offset[s]) mod T]`. Apply that one offset to the whole vector, including missingness, rather than choosing an offset anew each date or compressing away missing dates. Use only the fixed dev score index, never holdout observations.

Retain the original per-date required cohort and schedule. When a shifted value is absent/nonfinite for any required member, that draw is unavailable; do not alter the cohort, reuse a prior target, fill zero or redraw. Still emit all 500 draw records, with offsets or their verifiable deterministic recipe and explicit failure location/reason. Any unavailable draw prevents a complete p gate under this conservative contract. Such failure means the registered comparison could not be established, not that the economic null or strategy was rejected by a measured p-value.

An invented vector `[observed, observed, missing, missing]` on four scheduled dates, required on the first two dates, demonstrates the severity: each of the three nonzero shifts moves a missing score into at least one required date. The original observed comparison can therefore exist while all nonzero A draws are unavailable. This is predictable structural fragility in sparse or changing universes. It must be accepted or amended prospectively, not discovered and then cured by accepting only successful offsets.

Circular wrapping may assign a later dev score to an earlier date. That is allowed only as the declared randomization operation: it is not a historically tradable signal or evidence of point-in-time availability. Original observed scores must still have admitted availability; source admission is not bypassed by labeling an observed strategy a null. Retain original source references and label the shifted value's synthetic attribution separately.

Independent nonzero offsets exclude the identity transformation and all transformations with a zero offset for any symbol. They do not automatically constitute the full circular-shift group or an exchangeable exact randomization test. Structural missingness, secular trends and independent shifts that disrupt cross-symbol dependence add further assumptions. Preserve the original gate as a registered placebo screen with these qualifications; do not call it an exact calibrated type-I-error guarantee without an additional argument. A fixed seed provides reproducibility, not inferential validity.

For both families, bind the original scored statistic, tail direction, tie inclusion and finite-simulation p formula only after targeted source audit establishes their exact definitions. No source audit of that inference implementation was performed here; no replacement statistic or plus-one formula is adopted. Retain the original worse-of-two-families <= 0.05 threshold, four-cell and cumulative multiplicity accounting, and all 500 records per family. Undefined observed/null statistics or an incomplete family remain unavailable, never zero or a successful-only denominator. A conditional placebo label is not sufficient to admit a scientific p gate: the formula and randomization assumptions still need explicit source-supported review before that gate can be evaluated. This is a bounded remaining inference dependency, not a request for a new inference platform.

## Alternatives and decision boundary

No automatic less-conservative A substitution is recommended. Restricting shifts to offsets that happen to preserve required coverage conditions the null and changes its sampling law. Shifting only observed entries compresses economic time. Moving the cohort together with the scores changes the comparison universe and trading opportunity set. Restricting to complete-history symbols changes the registered cohort, breadth and possibly P1. Block/regime shifts introduce new block choices and a different null. Each is a plausible separately specified research design, but none is a harmless implementation fix or an authorized fallback after inspecting draw availability or financial results.

The precise prospective choice is therefore: register the conservative full-index A with retained unavailable draws and accept that it can block the complete placebo gate, or defer A/financial admission until a separately justified null and its unchanged-question implications are reviewed. Pure invented-input rank, endpoint and null-transform tests can proceed after these explicit contracts are frozen; preserving the existing rounding and short-first tie priority is recommended over the unadopted floor alternative. Actual source timing, P0/P1/P2, publication/eligibility, funding completeness and irreversible trial admission remain independent blockers. No threshold, four-cell count, holdout rule or historical evidence was changed by this review.
