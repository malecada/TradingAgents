## Why one investigation is proportionate

The proposed future sources have one concrete immediate purpose: reconstruct the already specified short-straddle/perpetual policy over one whole holding interval. The accounting mechanism, eight alternatives and missing-data rules can be frozen before collection. No additional current snapshot has an identified independent scientific value. The finite offline prototype now resolves the main cash, timing and practical episode-length engineering questions; remaining source availability uncertainty can be represented within the full intended denominator.

A separately granted source experiment followed by a separately granted financial child would require two cumulative allowance decisions, two run boundaries and later input bindings. That separation is defensible when collection itself answers an independent question or the eventual financial policy is genuinely unspecified. Here it mainly delays permission to execute the same intended analysis. It would also risk leaving a costly multiweek collection with no admitted use or encouraging a result-informed child after the observations exist.

One combined investigation does not count collection as free research or create a family alias. The original `options-volatility` family object remains unchanged at budget four/prior one. Preserve the known historical bundle, twelve RVIV rows, three new options claims and unknown wider multiplicity. A separately reviewed certificate may grant exactly one new named full-episode investigation at effective five. Thousands of source slots and eight correlated books remain explicitly enumerated subordinate observations/cases, not independent discoveries or uncounted follow-ups. No sixth allowance or rerun is implied.

## Required narrow protocol

1. **Freeze the full scientific and operational design before the future sample begins.** Bind the source and financial code, deterministic two-stage contract selection, unit/rule admission, minimum quantities, all eight cases, hourly action clocks, hedge/funding ownership, fees, wallet assumptions, missingness, benchmarks, descriptive exposure/uncertainty scope and fixed exit rule. Bind the absolute observation envelope, deterministic selected-calendar rule, source slots, output grammar and resource/attempt limits. Commit, back up and verify the source before claim and network access.
2. **Create an honest pre-window claim under an additive prospective protocol.** Existing design/metadata inputs and future observed market samples are distinct. The claim names a future observation window and acknowledges unresolved observed bytes. It must not invent a past dataset window or weaken the frozen original runtime's ready-only-after-window rule. Document and independently test this new protocol as an explicit addition to the existing freeze-and-bind semantics.
3. **Collect only the frozen source slots.** Use the bounded immutable journal proposed in the history review: durable intent before request, one attempt per slot, immediate raw/prefix preservation, exact identity/clock/byte accounting, no replay of uncertain intents, retained missed slots and explicit active-only restart. Contract selection consumes only the predeclared index/metadata criteria and never reselects after size, premium or later performance is observed. Source quality reports may diagnose retention and availability; no interim cash, hedge-performance or significance looks are authorized.
4. **Seal and independently admit the source phase.** At the frozen end, publish an immutable manifest over every intended slot, receipt, partial and raw member. Reconstruct the selected calendar and semantic/clock admission independently before any financial evaluation. This seal is an internal phase artifact, not a terminal `complete.json` or `failed.json` that may later be reopened. The whole-episode claim remains active through its permitted analysis phase.
5. **Bind the observed bytes and evaluate the frozen eight cases once.** Exact manifest/input hashes must refer to the predeclared names, paths, datasets, transformation and future window. No binding may change selection, costs, duration or missing-data rules. Record a durable evaluation intent before computation. Interrupted computation follows an explicit pre-frozen failure/recovery rule; it must not silently become permission for a new empirical attempt. Preserve partial outputs and all unavailable cases. Close the claim only through the independently verified terminal protocol.

Source completion and financial availability need separate component statuses. A fully accounted source collection may yield unavailable cash cases. Conversely, plausible retained cash files do not repair an operational timeout or missing terminal verification. The existing spread failure demonstrates why these distinctions must remain explicit. A data-quality failure must not trigger a replacement episode or an extra source allowance under the original claim.

## Portability addendum: authoritative local claim and bounded capture worker

**Approve the proposed separation of authority as the simpler deployment architecture.** The local consolidated checkout retains the sole authoritative claim, grant, complete nineteen-terminal history, phase transitions, financial analysis and final terminal receipt. A dedicated VPS process receives a hash-bound copy of that already created claim and a frozen source-worker package. It has authority only to collect the explicitly assigned source slots and publish its journal/source seal. It has no admission, budget-extension, financial-evaluation or outer-terminal authority. This architecture decision itself authorizes no remote changes.

This revises the earlier suggestion that the VPS necessarily needs a complete independent research run root with all historical Git/raw/ML dependencies. A subordinate worker does not need those dependencies if the local controller performs and records complete verified admission before delegation. The VPS must not be presented as a second independently admitted run root. The copied claim, grant hash and parent-history certificate hash are references to the controller's proof; their presence alone does not reproduce that proof on the worker.

The minimum contract is:

1. **One externally anchored assignment.** The controller freezes and verifies the exact package manifest, copied claim hash, grant hash, historical proof reference, worker identity, source files, runtime requirement, request/selection recipes, observation envelope and resource limits. The reviewed launch supplies the expected package/claim identity independently of mutable bundle contents. Merely hashing a manifest supplied beside its own files is insufficient. Initial local claim creation precedes all VPS market requests.
2. **One bounded worker and journal.** Use one dedicated research directory and persistent lock on the assigned host. The worker verifies its package/source/claim before entry or controlled resume; it cannot choose another claim, journal root, host, expiry, symbol or policy to recover from failure. Deterministic source selection may run there only as frozen capture logic and must publish its immutable selection record. No automatic failover or second worker is part of this scope. A stdlib-only package plus an isolated verified Python 3.13 runtime is sufficient if its actual dependency inventory confirms that claim. The reported existing Python 3.12 installation does not itself satisfy the pinned 3.13 requirement.
3. **Explicit quiescence before authoritative closure.** The controller must not publish an outer terminal while a remote worker may still issue requests under its copied authorization. Require an acknowledged stop/source seal, verified process death or expiry of a frozen bounded worker lease. If the worker is unreachable, retain a nonterminal interruption or stop-pending record until that condition is established. Do not close failed locally and later reopen it to accept continued capture. Worker sealing remains a source-phase event, never a substitute for the local terminal.
4. **Verified return into staging.** Preserve the remote journal and return it to a new local staging location under the frozen path/member grammar and byte/count caps. Reject symlinks, traversal, unexpected members, truncation and hash mismatches. Verify every raw/partial/intent/receipt/recovery/spec/seal member against the manifest and independently reconstruct the complete source denominator and clocks. Do not overwrite either the remote original or existing local evidence. A manifest-only copy is not a raw-data backup or completed transfer.
5. **Local admission and analysis once.** After worker quiescence and complete transfer, the authoritative controller revalidates history/source identities, binds the captured manifest under the frozen episode contract and independently admits source semantics. It alone records the analysis intent, computes the eight books once and issues the outer terminal. A returned seal cannot authorize altered costs, selected contracts, dates, missingness rules, reruns or an additional allowance.

This contract removes duplicate admission roots and cross-host financial-analysis races while retaining the full historical proof. Actual raw recoverability requires demonstrated transfer/backup of the raw members; a compact worker package or configured remote is not that evidence. The final checkpoint must distinguish the worker's original capture store, any verified local copy and any verified external backup.

The reported always-on VPS, available tmux/screen and free disk are useful setup facts, not proof that this worker package has been installed, admitted or launched. Prepare and verify the isolated package under the existing manual route. No systemd edits, production reuse, credentials inspection or implicit recurring job is part of this architecture. Exact execution ownership, stop behavior and any launch authority must be explicit in the concrete setup record.
