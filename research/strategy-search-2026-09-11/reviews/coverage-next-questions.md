data README and EOHSummary/BTC catalogues. The README documents general archive
delivery/checksums and spot/futures schemas, not EOHSummary semantics. Current
catalogue presence and a complete lexical CSV demonstrate source existence;
they do not establish hourly timing, quote simultaneity or 2023 product units.
`reviews/options-eoh-semantic-followup.md` records four targeted official-domain
queries after the candidate-header finding; none resolved the missing meanings.
Repeated broad namespace searches or additional days do not provide a new

A specifically identified primary schema/version document would justify a
bounded documentary review. No such new uninspected authoritative target is
established by this coverage audit. Current option endpoints can document a
new prospective schema, but cannot silently retrofit it to a 2023 archive or
prove seller access/fees/affordability. Even a genuine hourly best-quote snapshot
would remain insufficient for continuous dynamic-hedge fills and tail margin.
Do not infer “public options impossible”; keep the precise historical-route
dependency and the remaining single allowance visible.

## Triangle: a third frequency/latency question needs a useful endpoint

The saved single asynchronous capture does not estimate opportunity frequency
or timeless absence. A finite prospective source/persistence experiment could
be distinct from a fee rescue if it freezes observation duration/cadence, both
cycles, unchanged cost assumptions, full missing/denied/gap denominator and
visible-size constraints before seeing quotes. Its endpoint should be whether
apparently cost-qualified conditions persist across conservatively aligned
observations—not a backtest that assumes three atomic fills.

However, sparse REST polling cannot establish subsecond persistence or local
matching-engine opportunity duration. A websocket implementation would require
sequenced book reconstruction, snapshot reconciliation, clock/latency evidence
and synthetic gap recovery; visible size is still no fill guarantee. With only
one question left, source implementation plus empirical capture must be a
single predeclared bounded admission question, not hidden uncounted probes.
No orders/paper start are authorized. Given current infrastructure uncertainty
and no quantified expected opportunity frequency, this is lower priority than
the inexpensive news inventory and finishing the already prepared WBETH book.
Do not build a trading stack just to use the last triangle slot.
