## Charter 5, the last attempt

Charter 5 failed with t −0.08 over 104 weeks: pairwise comparisons of text dossiers carry no information on
next week's return rank beyond the quant forecast, price controls and news counts.

**Design.** Weekly top-50 perpetual contracts, 104 test weeks from 2023-01-06 to 2024-12-27. A public news
archive (CoinDesk Data feed dump on Hugging Face, 67 extra outlets, CC-BY-NC-4.0) was added for assets other
than BTC and ETH. Each asset got a seven-day text dossier with all names replaced by tokens. Two analyst agents
compared pairs of assets, an adjudicator settled disagreements, and code turned the picks into one
Bradley-Terry score per asset and week. Model: `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` through OpenRouter.

| Measure | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gate G1: Newey-West t, needed ≥ 2.0 | −0.08 |
| Gate G2: mean by year, both needed > 0 | 2023 −0.020, 2024 +0.017 |
| Same score without the news-count controls | t +0.22 |
| News counts alone, beyond everything else | t +1.89, not gated |
| Asset-weeks with a dossier | 2,892 of 5,200 (56 %, 48 % without the archive) |
| Comparisons | 14,200, 73.7 % decided, agents agree on 88 % |
| Model named the hidden asset | 38.9 % of dossiers (chance 0.5 %) |
| Cost of Charter 5 | $7.61 |

Where the model recognised the asset, its picks leaned towards the assets that then did better (+0.045
against −0.022). That is the sign of the model remembering the period, not a usable signal.

Records: charter `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-llm-v2-charter5-direct-comparison-charter.md` (frozen,
