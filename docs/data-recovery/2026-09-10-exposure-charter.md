# Original-book lifecycle exposure forensic audit

The official closure notices reveal a separate readiness issue: five terminated contracts were members of the original liquidation-fade monthly universes. Existing nonmissing, frozen hourly bars do not establish executable prices after forced settlement. Membership alone cannot identify actual holdings, so a separate exposure-only audit is registered before reconstruction.

`audit_lifecycle_exposure_2026_09_10` freezes all six original cells, all five events, the original 217 hourly inputs and monthly universe, and the unchanged signal/weight construction. It consumes original inputs only, including their disclosed holes; it does not consume recovered prices. The audit reconstructs target allocations solely to locate lifecycle assumptions in the preserved September 9 measurements. No PnL, performance gate, probe statistic, model fit, selection or holdout is permitted.

Hourly observations carry UTC open labels. A close labelled `t-1h` becomes available at `t`; the book applies `W[t]` using that mark and values the position at `t+1h`. The diagnostic reports allocation during a bar containing termination, incoming allocation at an exact boundary event (even if the new target is flat), and post-termination requested allocation. It cannot manufacture a pre-event exit from a millisecond bar-close label. New targets and target-weight increases after the announced restriction are reported separately; quantity changes from price/NAV drift cannot be inferred from targets alone.

Each of the six cells retains a record for each of the five events. A detected assumption qualifies the original measurement. An absent flag does not prove complete data, valid execution, final settlement cashflows or profitability. The original missing-input behavior is preserved only to reproduce those historical allocation assumptions; this is not an endorsement of treating a missing signal as an observed absence of an event.

The committed gate pins exact times, official announcement identifiers, original input inventory hash, universe hash, clocks, cell order, event slot size and cap. Clean committed code and unchanged inputs are required. Output is exclusive and immutable under `data/recovery/2026-09-10/exposure-audit/`. Six append-only records in the output’s dedicated `forensic-ledger.jsonl` retain the inspection denominator. The central predlab trial ledger stays byte-for-byte unchanged; no financial candidate is evaluated and no trial or DSR count changes. This pre-execution clarification prevents nonfinancial inspections from inflating hypothesis counts. No accounting settlement extension or performance replay is authorized by this forensic gate.
