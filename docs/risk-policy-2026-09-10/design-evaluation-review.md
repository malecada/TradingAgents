# Independent evaluation design review — factor risk policies

Date: September 10, 2026. This document reviews a bounded evaluation contract before new outcomes and incorporates the parent's final evaluation decisions. It does not register a gate or authorize execution by itself. Only methodology, existing registrations/ledger metadata, source and the completed diagnostic interpretation were inspected; no performance, resampling, signal or new policy calculation was run.

## Scope and estimand

The proposed 2×2 design is suitable for a mechanism comparison on the already examined development history, provided the policy axes are specified precisely. Preserve all eighteen factor identities, original causal signals and target-direction state, development sizing warmup, price snapshots, accounting assumptions and the permanent 15% drawdown latch. The four arms are:

| Arm | Position magnitude | Response after a price stop |
|---|---|---|
| A00 | Original entry/flip sizing retained | Original immediate saved-target re-entry |
| A10 | Daily causal volatility resizing | Original immediate saved-target re-entry |
| A01 | Original entry/flip sizing retained | Wait for a new original target-direction episode |
| A11 | Daily causal volatility resizing | Wait for a new original target-direction episode |

The primary object remains each configuration's daily mean of its separately simulated BTC and ETH simple returns. This is a benchmark index, not a pooled executable account. Retain each asset's full book and dollars separately. Do not pool their dollar charges or interpret the eighteen overlapping configurations as independent observations.

The design can establish what the specified policies do on the fixed saved price path. It cannot produce fresh validation, restore a spent holdout, establish a best policy across untested choices, or attribute every improvement to lower fees. Changed size and re-entry necessarily alter exposure, subsequent stops and halt dates. Lower Kelly and removal of volatility targeting already appear in the historical sizing record and must not be presented as new ideas.

## Definitions that must be frozen before implementation

1. **Daily resizing changes magnitude only.** Use the same confidence-one formula and causal sigma252 as the baseline: magnitude `min(3, 0.15 / sigma252)` for a permitted nonzero original target direction. Use the original twenty-return volatility history and development reset; do not replace sqrt252 by sqrt365 inside sizing. The 95th-percentile volatility gate continues to govern original builder entries/flips only. Turning that gate into a daily liquidation rule would add another policy axis. Missing/nonpositive required volatility must be unavailable or an explicitly preserved warmup state, never an implicit hold or zero chosen after results.
2. **Stop state must not regenerate the signal builder.** Derive original target-direction episodes from the frozen original latent target, not from executed positions or resized magnitudes. An episode starts when its direction changes, including flat-to-nonzero. At a price stop, remember the stopped episode; suppress its subsequent exposure until a strictly later nonzero original episode. A later opposite target may enter immediately at that later decision; a genuine intervening flat episode permits a later same-sign episode. If the intended rule instead requires an opposite direction specifically, choose that definition explicitly before registration. Do not count same-sign resizing as a fresh episode.
3. **Order matters.** A stop on a bar cannot be used to alter that bar's earlier decision or to re-enter later within the same daily bar. A simultaneous permanent halt dominates any rearm and remains absorbing. Preserve the original price-stop anchor through same-sign resizing; a real fresh entry or sign flip sets a new anchor. Explicitly test any engine bookkeeping that resets trade-equity state when target magnitude changes.
4. **Daily maintenance is a real order.** Size from pretrade NAV and charge turnover against incoming marked holdings, including same-target drift. Preserve fees, spread, slippage and quadratic impact. Do not treat resize turnover as free or sum absolute target-change and maintenance components without their netting term.

These definitions distinguish refreshing risk from restarting a target episode without altering the factor signals, hold rule, original early-exit logic or permanent halt. Their final wording should be reconciled with the independent engine review before a gate is committed.

## Fixed reporting and paired contrasts

Report all 72 primary configuration/arm rows and all 144 sleeves in fixed original order. For each, report full-clock mean simple return, annualized arithmetic mean, Sharpe using sqrt365 and ddof1 with zero hurdle, compounded total return, maximum drawdown including initial NAV, realized return volatility, active/waiting/halted dates, first halt and post-halt cash count. Zero-variance or invalid ratios are unavailable rather than assigned an attractive value.

For each sleeve retain applied/incoming/closing exposures; integrated absolute exposure and signed exposure; risk-proxy quantiles and excess-risk counts with finite/active/full-clock denominators; sizing age; price stops and fill-envelope exceptions; stop successors and blocked re-entry opportunities; turnover, gross PnL, signed assumed funding, linear execution charges and impact. Charges by mutually exclusive opening/maintenance/flip/resize/exit categories must reconcile to total charges. Re-entry charges are a subset, not an additional category to add again.

The fixed direct contrasts are A10−A00, A01−A00 and A11−A00 within each of eighteen configurations: **54 contrasts**. Pair by the entire identical calendar, including unequal halt/cash periods. Report differences in all headline metrics and absolute risk/exposure/cost quantities. A candidate's different active clock is an outcome, not an excuse to change the comparison denominator. Per-active or per-turnover ratios can supplement the full-clock results only with explicit numerators and denominators.

Also report the factorial effects for each metric: resizing `[(A10−A00)+(A11−A01)]/2`, episode waiting `[(A01−A00)+(A11−A10)]/2`, and interaction `A11−A10−A01+A00`. They are descriptive contrasts across deterministic policy arms, not eighteen independent replications or three additional hidden confirmatory families. Do not rank configurations, write BEST, or select only the two previously favorable examples.

Use three fixed descriptive periods: November 8, 2021–December 31, 2022; 2023–2024; and January 1–March 31, 2025. Preserve cash in all period metrics, and report their complete counts. A period entirely in cash is not an independent replication of success.

## Frozen statistical and reporting decision

Use two separate statuses. **Engineering conformance** requires exact intended transitions, complete calendars, baseline parity and accounting reconciliation. Daily resizing should satisfy the algebraic current-risk cap on every permitted active sizing decision; episode waiting should have zero entries inside a blocked old episode. These are deterministic correctness checks, not evidence of economic benefit. **Economic comparison** reports the full fixed set of paired outcomes as retrospective descriptive diagnostics; it has no adoption PASS or formal fresh-validation p-value.

**No bootstrap, formal p-value, Holm discovery decision, DSR or adoption gate is included in this cycle.** Absorbing halts and long cash tails challenge stationarity, and resampling already realized returns would not preserve the original state transition process. Replaying policies on resampled prices would add a different experiment. Full-history and fixed-period exact differences, along with risk/exposure/cost trade-offs, are the reporting contract. Point ΔSharpe is descriptive; there is no bootstrap ΔSharpe interval or search over inferential methods.

No arbitrary new Sharpe/effect floor should be reverse-engineered from the latest factor results. Record whether each arm improves net mean, drawdown and the intended mechanism, but avoid a composite “pass” that can be reached by choosing a preferred metric. Any simple comparison flags must be explicitly descriptive and paired with the complete values. Exposure suppression can reduce losses, stops and the risk proxy by keeping the book flat; it must not be counted as validated improvement. Report active days and integrated exposure so that this trade-off remains visible. DSR is not an adoption shortcut: the audit row count does not establish effective independence, and combining incomparable historical SRs does not supply a valid null maximum. No winner-selection rule is introduced.

## Costs and convention forensics

For each of the 72 configuration/arm identities, independently simulate the fixed four assumptions: primary, zero execution costs, double execution costs and zero assumed funding. Execution multipliers cover fee, slippage, spread and impact; funding remains +3bp/day signed under its original opening-exposure approximation except in the zero-funding variant. Preserve the 3% threshold price stop and permanent 15% halt in every arm/variant; each path determines its own stops and halt dates. Never calculate alternative costs by subtracting charges from a primary net-return stream and calling that a replay.

This yields **288 two-sleeve book/index evaluations and 576 sleeve books**, including 72 primary evaluations. Keep them nested under the 72 identities. Reconcile all four A00 assumptions against the existing corresponding corrected traces and returns; these artifacts are present in the old output namespace. Report all sensitivities, including failures, with no choice of the most favorable cost assumption. Lower zero-cost performance is possible through changed state and must not be repaired or suppressed.

Save **72 invalid-log shadows**, one per primary configuration/arm. Replace only the frozen primary gross simple-return term by exposure×log1p(effective simple mark return), retaining the same primary exposure, stop schedule and fee/funding fractions. Explicitly label this as invalid accounting on a fixed schedule, not an executable policy alternative. Preserve undefined cases. Report when accounting convention changes the sign or ordering of a comparison; never select the log version. Do not add log-based stateful replays, different fill models, funding estimates, volatility targets, cooldown lengths or halt resets to this grid.

Before real execution, use synthetic conformance/negative controls: A00 parity; future-price perturbations cannot change earlier decisions; resize without resetting price-stop anchors; same-sign magnitude changes cannot rearm; flat/new-opposite episodes and permanent-halt priority; a stop cannot re-enter in its own bar; all-cash and untriggered-policy arms exactly match; long/short funding and fee signs; drift/netting; cap clipping; invalid/missing clock; initial loss/drawdown; full cash tails; and a planted path whose known resizing/re-entry trade-off appears correctly. Resampled strategy placebos are not needed to assert a deterministic policy mechanism and should not silently expand this comparison. Any new alpha or adoption claim would require its own causal nulls and power design.

## Existing code: reuse boundaries

- `tradingagents/rebuild/compare.py` jointly resamples two streams, but silently uses an inner non-NaN intersection, sqrt252 and a zero-SR convention for zero variance. Its `p_pos` is not a null-centered p-value. It should not be directly reused as the reporting contract.
- `scripts/bootstrap_sharpe.py` includes replay imports, active-only per-coin selections, a 4.5% cash hurdle, sqrt252 and independently seeded comparator sampling. It is unsuitable for this bounded full-clock paired design.
- `tradingagents/predlab/meanstats.py` offers a stationary-bootstrap primitive, but lacks the required complete-clock/finite/degeneracy admission layer; `p_pos` is the share of positive draws, not a test p-value.
- `tradingagents/predlab/pp.py` drops NaNs in `ann_sr`/`dsr`; its cross-trial estimator cannot replace exact calendars or a declared historical trial policy. `registry.trial_identity` includes experiment/cell/model/config/window, so policy axes must appear in the identity and all attempted configurations must be logged.
- The saved-forecast bootstrap supplies an efficient geometric-block summation pattern, but its loss/mask/eligibility rules are forecast-specific. No bootstrap is included in the final risk-policy design. No old generic helper or prior experiment main should be invoked for a new unregistered calculation.
