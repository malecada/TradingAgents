# Sizing and stop re-entry: design-history review

September 10, 2026. This review inspected existing registrations, trial identities, source and the closed-program memory index. No new strategy result, return calculation, simulation, market request or validation-window access occurred.

**The proposed fixed 2×2 is not an exact repeat of an identified registered experiment.** Related sizing and stop variants have been tried extensively. Novelty is limited to the specified interaction between refreshing causal volatility sizing and refusing renewed exposure after a price stop until the chosen direction-change condition occurs. This absence claim is confined to the reviewed records, rather than every historical branch or undocumented trial. The family remains development research with zero validated strategies.

## Definitions needed before outcomes

1. **Choose the direction clock explicitly.** Saved target direction, raw factor signal and executed holding direction are different states. `v2_sizing.build_positions_with_hold` ignores a flat signal after the minimum hold; long-only targets can remain positive indefinitely. If the gate requires an opposite nonzero saved-target direction after a price stop, some long-only sleeves may remain flat for the rest of the window. That is a foreseeable policy outcome to retain, not grounds for a later flat-reset exception. Specify whether zero counts as a change, whether a zero-to-same-direction transition unlocks, when the stop direction is recorded, and the earliest later decision eligible to unlock.
2. **Separate sizing from signal/entry state.** Daily refresh should specify the same strictly lagged twenty-return volatility, sqrt252 scaling, Kelly/confidence/leverage algebra and unavailable-volatility behavior. Decide before results whether the existing 95th-percentile volatility gate applies only to original entries or also to maintained/reopened exposure. Reapplying it daily would add a second timing mechanism. Retain the original hold/early-exit signal schedule unless its change is expressly part of the registered factor. Same-sign size changes should not silently reset price-stop entry reference or alter entry/hold age.
3. **Preserve actual trade accounting.** Daily target refresh creates signed trades against drifted holdings, including costs when magnitude changes without a direction change. Net simultaneous target-change and maintenance trades before computing actual turnover. Reopening a stopped position uses its real new entry reference and incurs its entry costs. Preserve the 3% price-stop threshold and daily fill/funding qualifications; do not label the spot/mixed-provider cache as verified perpetual execution data.
4. **Keep the permanent 15% halt and full calendar.** Apply the unchanged rule independently to every arm, since changed costs and exposure can change halt dates. Do not freeze the incumbent halt schedule, relax the threshold, restart after a halt or select on active-only performance. Include initial NAV, warmup, all stopped/blocked/flat dates and post-halt cash. A lower drawdown achieved by suppressing exposure is not automatically a superior signal.
5. **Freeze the comparison denominator.** Four policies across eighteen configurations imply 72 configuration-arm records and 144 BTC/ETH sleeve paths if all are executed. Preserve duplicates, long-only lockouts, unavailable cases and zero-variance books. Define whether inference concerns three contrasts against the incumbent or factorial main effects/interaction; register any family adjustment and selection rule in advance. The two coins and overlapping configurations are not independent trials. A selected winner cannot be reported as a fresh out-of-sample discovery.

## Spent windows and a prospective route

The November 7, 2021–March 31, 2025 factor development period has already been used for selection, accounting correction and mechanism inspection. The new 2×2 can estimate a conditional policy comparison there under a new charter; it cannot convert that history into validation data.

The factor holdout **April 1, 2025–July 1, 2026 is spent**. Evidence is explicit in `data/rebuild/frozen_portfolio.json:holdout_protocol`, `data/rebuild/gates.json:holdout_deploy`, the four `holdout_oneshot` ledger records at lines 70–73, and findings §41. The factor-plus-carry one-shot recorded NO-GO. Later accounting qualification does not restore its virgin status. September's factor correction supersedes only the eighteen development measurements, as stated by correction-register records `2026-09-10-factor-floor-classic-scope-qualification` and `2026-09-10-fixed-factor-cycle-completed`.

July 2, 2026 onward must not be assumed fresh for a new factor policy merely because this exact experiment is absent. Part of that future-data period is already named in the separate `predlab_p6` forecast registration, whose earliest spend is July 2027 after at least twelve months; it is not a reusable factor holdout entitlement. Cross-venue history on the same market dates would likewise require an explicit contamination and instrument-provenance assessment.

The cleanest route is **prospective validation beginning only after the final policy and evaluation contract are committed**. Freeze one eventual policy or a fixed family-level test, criteria, duration, instrument/source identities, execution and funding coverage requirements, failure handling and no-retuning rule before its first evaluated decision. A twelve-month minimum is a reasonable planning option consistent with the existing deferred program, but no duration or new gate is registered by this review. Start at the first complete UTC return interval whose opening decision follows the freeze; prior observations may supply declared causal warmup, not a secretly scored validation segment. Keep the halt permanent within that window. A fresh deployment-state initialization at a genuinely new window must be declared before data, following the historical fresh-latch convention; it is not permission to restart mid-window.

No prospective collector, paper account, scheduler, deployment or holdout spend was started. A development improvement would justify considering that separately registered validation step, not implementation in live trading.
