# Public funding source policy

This policy supports current S1 paper measurement only. The default capture spans nine UTC calendar dates: midnight eight dates before the captured exchange date through one fixed server-time cutoff, including the partial current date. It does not reopen historical settlement investigations, reconstruct past eligibility, start paper trading or change a research verdict.

The source is Binance USD-M public REST. All requests are explicit GETs without credentials. The verified endpoint contracts are:

| Endpoint | Capture interpretation |
|---|---|
| [`/fapi/v1/fundingRate`](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-History) | Published funding events; inclusive millisecond bounds, ascending order, maximum 1,000 rows. Preserve rate, funding time, associated mark and event type. Always specify symbol, bounds and limit; documentation describes inconsistent unbounded defaults. |
| [`/fapi/v1/fundingInfo`](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-Info) | Current adjusted-symbol intervals, caps and floors; no historical effective-time query. Shares 500 requests per five minutes per IP with funding history despite weight zero. |
| [`/fapi/v1/premiumIndex`](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Mark-Price) | Current rate/mark and announced next funding time, with provider observation time. It is not a replacement for a published event. |
| [`/fapi/v1/time`](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Check-Server-Time) | Clock used to freeze the capture cutoff. |
| [`/fapi/v1/exchangeInfo`](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Exchange-Information) | Current instrument identities, assets, underlying classification, lifecycle dates, status and filters. Its embedded server time is not the capture clock. |

Each run preserves immutable response bodies, request parameters, HTTP outcomes, UTC request/receipt times, relevant response headers, hashes and transformation source identity. Event time, provider observation time and first local availability remain distinct. A later retrieval does not prove that a record or schedule was available before an earlier decision. Conflicting observations within a run are quarantined, including conflicts split across a full page's final timestamp. Later captures remain separate; automatic reconciliation of revisions across runs is not implemented. Interrupted HTTP bodies retain partial bytes, known status and headers. Exchange time must be within five minutes of receipt time; selected current schedule observations must be at most five minutes old.

Current admission requires `status=TRADING`, `contractType=PERPETUAL`, and both quote and margin assets equal to USDT. Full underlying and lifecycle metadata are retained. Explicitly requested unavailable instruments remain in the denominator; neither successors nor historical membership are inferred. `Regular` is supported. The compatibility policy accepts a missing `rateType` only when captured metadata identifies `underlyingType=COIN`, labeling it `unspecified_crypto`; it is not silently relabeled `Regular`. `Special`, other unsupported types and ambiguous missing types are quarantined.

**Query completeness and economic completeness are different.** Successful bounded pagination establishes exhaustion of the returned history for that query. It does not prove that every economically due event was published, that every schedule change was observed, or that a particular account was charged. Empty responses, missing boundary events, failed requests and unsupported transitions never become zero funding. Rate limits apply across processes sharing the IP; collection stops on rate-limit responses rather than bypassing them. [Official limits](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/derivatives-trading-usds-futures/general-info).

Schedules can be 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours and can change within a day. Cap/floor settlements can trigger 8/4-to-1-hour changes, reflected approximately 15 minutes later without a separate announcement. [May 2025 rule](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/3243c81a35bd4f0c86a37315c3dc96cc). Sixteen qualifying hourly cycles can now trigger a four-hour interval on the next cycle. [January 2026 revision](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/e4445d0389ce4defa6009021fcf6ee46). Two-hour schedules are also documented. [Official example](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/6e6d1f52906a4bc79467c876e7da9089). Current metadata cannot be projected backward to certify these schedules. The retained seven-day cadence inference is explicitly qualified; unobserved faster events remain a limitation.

Positive rates are paid by longs; negative rates reverse the transfer. Exact cash funding depends on held quantity and the associated mark, and actual account charging can deviate from the scheduled time by 15 seconds. [Funding FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/360033525031). The existing paper approximation remains unchanged: sum rates over `[D 00:00, D+1 00:00)`, require the following boundary for its cadence check, and retain signed daily funding accounting. This is not event-marked account cashflow reconciliation. Missing marks cannot be repaired with later prices, and a captured history alone does not establish execution readiness.

The snapshot reader applies the separately committed [timestamp amendment](2026-09-10-timestamp-amendment.md): raw labels from an exact UTC hour through one second later may receive a derived hourly comparison label. The bound is an engineering policy for observed millisecond jitter, not a provider guarantee. Raw daily membership remains unchanged; off-grid or ambiguous mappings remain unavailable. The legacy store reader is unchanged.
