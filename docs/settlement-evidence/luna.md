## Historical rule and the mark-price alternative

The [December 24, 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20211224102630id_/https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/dd60dfbf654d4055aa6b217ea6d5ddba) and [May 25, 2022](https://web.archive.org/web/20220525030936id_/https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/dd60dfbf654d4055aa6b217ea6d5ddba) copies are Internet Archive mirrors of Binance pages, not independent exchange records. Their matching decoded body hash is `9aacdea97d52866685b27ea7976d3b6a5ac43782cf1bfbad951b8340c5fd04de`. Bracketing strengthens the default-rule evidence but cannot exclude an intervening emergency change. The [November 2024 notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/4bcabddf0e81423ebca242e185bf157d), preserved by the BNX investigation, explicitly changes delivery and delisted-contract averaging from one hour to thirty minutes effective November 11, 2024 at 08:00 UTC. Today's thirty-minute rule is therefore unsuitable as a silent LUNA assumption.

An account statement is **not the only acceptable price evidence**. A historically applicable rule plus authentic complete required observations and rounding could establish the price. Alternatively, an authoritative exact terminal mark, proven to be the mark used by the settlement procedure at its effective time, could avoid reconstructing every second. Existing candles supply neither that event-to-mark mapping nor the timestamp of the individual observation used at closure. A candle opening at 15:30 labels its minute; it does not certify a particular tick as the completed settlement mark.

## Public API and export routes

The [May 2, 2022 archived official API reference](https://web.archive.org/web/20220502133908id_/https://binance-docs.github.io/apidocs/futures/en/) predates LUNA closure by ten days. `/fapi/v1/premiumIndex` accepts a symbol, not a historical timestamp. `estimatedSettlePrice` and websocket `P` are estimates; the live one-second mark stream is not an archive. A December 2020 changelog entry predates those fields to the event. Public `allForceOrders` was retired in April 2021, so that route was not queried.

Ordinary signed `/fapi/v1/income` documents three-month retention and the historical category spelling `DELIVERED_SETTELMENT`, alongside `REALIZED_PNL`, `COMMISSION` and `FUNDING_FEE`. This does not establish retention for the separate signed export endpoints `/fapi/v1/income/asyn` and `/income/asyn/id`, added in March 2022. The archived export section specifies five requests per month and 24-hour link expiry but no oldest-date limit. No account endpoint was called.

The [current official account reference](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/account) was readable through the web research service. It describes a maximum **one-year requested span**, five export requests per month, and seven-day link expiry. A maximum span is not a maximum historical lookback. No earliest retained date is stated there; availability of a May 2022 export today is **unknown**, not ruled out. Direct retrieval of its linked schema returned an empty HTTP 202, preserved in the manifest. The separate `current-export-web-observation.json` records the rendered-text observation, exact URL and short excerpts; exact service retrieval time/headers were not returned and are explicitly unavailable. This is supplementary documentation, not an admitted historical cashflow or a successfully archived current schema body.

## Observation coverage and conditional bounds

The official [LUNA index archive](https://data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/daily/indexPriceKlines/LUNAUSDT/1m/LUNAUSDT-1m-2022-05-12.zip) and its [published checksum](https://data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/daily/indexPriceKlines/LUNAUSDT/1m/LUNAUSDT-1m-2022-05-12.zip.CHECKSUM) were downloaded once. The checksum matches. Its CSV has **no header**, 1,440 unique sorted minute rows, and a complete May 12 UTC clock. The pre-closure API query was explicitly bounded to 14:30–15:29:59.999 UTC. The archive interval listing has one minute as its shortest interval and no one-second product. The shared BZRX top-level daily product inventory contains no settlement product. These inventories qualify the inspected public archive, not every possible private archive.

The historical index-kline schema labels zero-based field 8 **“Number of bisic data”**. In the sixty returned rows it is 60 in 53 rows, 59 in six, and 58 in one. Its units, sampling alignment and equivalence to the settlement input clock are not established. Those values are **not interpreted as an eight-second data gap**. Other zero fields are explicitly ignored by the schema. Independent of that ambiguity, OHLC aggregation is insufficient to identify the exact average.

Minute lows and highs could constrain a mean only after proving the historical rule, observation-series identity, complete coverage of every required sample, sampling weights and any applicable protection/rounding. Those conditions are presently incomplete. No numeric bound on the **actual** LUNA settlement is admitted, and no midpoint or average of minute closes is substituted. The original trade tail and post-closure mark/index continuation do not repair that evidentiary gap.

## Funding evidence and remaining request

The [April 2, 2022 archived funding FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20220402121500id_/https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/360033525031/) precedes the event and states the valuation relation **“Nominal Value of Positions = Mark Price x Size of a Contract”**. It describes signed transfers, no Binance fee on the transfer itself, and the default eight-hour clock. Positions closed before a funding time owe and receive nothing at that time. The page also notes up to fifteen seconds of funding transaction-time deviation. This is historical general evidence, not proof of a LUNA-specific special adjustment. Its embedded publication metadata and absent update field are recorded; the Wayback capture date, rather than a missing update timestamp, establishes the observed historical version.

If the announced 15:30 termination completed under this default, a position terminated then would not survive to the scheduled 16:00 funding event. That is a conditional rule implication; public funding rows continuing after closure do not prove otherwise. The final relevant valuation and any special adjustment remain requested. Momentum and liquidation-fade's explicitly funding-excluded historical measurement contracts must not silently become all-in calculations; the carry book requires funding-event valuations rather than a clipped sum of daily rates.

The [unsent provider request](/home/malecada/master_thesis/TradingAgents-audit-fixes/docs/settlement-evidence/provider-request-draft.md) adequately requests the missing LUNA fields: authoritative terminal price/time or applicable exact terminal mark and rule; contract multiplier and rounding; emergency exceptions; automatic-settlement commission schedule/classification; final funding event valuation and special adjustment; and event ordering. An authorized historical export remains a possible route, with availability untested. No message or authenticated request was sent.
