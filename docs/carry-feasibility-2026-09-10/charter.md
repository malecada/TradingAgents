# Dated carry feasibility — fixed public measurement

The user approved the proposed order on September 10, 2026: economic feasibility, a complete hedge accounting example, then a separately registered strategy evaluation only if justified. The objective is positive net returns with little crypto-price exposure. Intended capital is initially approximately 1,000 and potentially 10,000; Binance is available according to the user, Bitrue is a possible alternative. No account credentials or private state are required for this stage.

This is a current public-book measurement and synthetic accounting exercise, not a historical backtest, a realized return series or an adoption test. The prior 820-row financial ledger and old gate objects remain unchanged. Every source response, failure and scenario is retained under a new directory and separate measurement ledger. The old spot/perpetual carry verdict and spent windows remain preserved; its percentage-return and equity-drift machinery are not reused.

## Fixed scope before quotes

- Binance USD-M linear dated BTC/ETH contracts, quoted and margined in USDT, paired with the same venue's BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT spot. Stablecoin-denominated capital scenarios are 1,000 and 10,000 USDT. These are not verified USD/EUR conversion proceeds. Fiat conversion, stablecoin and withdrawal economics remain separate admission requirements.
- Inventory all BTC/ETH contracts from public metadata. Per asset select the earliest active nonperpetual eligible expiry 7 through 180 days from the initial exchange clock; ties use instrument ID. This is a calendar rule, never a highest-yield selection. Freeze selected IDs across the capture. Missing, unsupported or absent instruments retain rows.
- Bitrue is a documentation/product-availability comparison. No substitute perpetual is evaluated as a dated future. A verified same-currency linear dated product would require a pre-result source/adapter amendment before its quote measurement. Other venues are outside the user's current scope.
- No inverse or coin-collateral payoff, multi-asset collateral, cross-venue hedge, borrowing, funding-bearing long-dated perpetual, margin offsets, automated order or account action. Current metadata is not historical contract evidence.
- Three scheduled quote batches at offsets 0, 30 and 60 seconds after initial metadata admission. Use concurrent public spot/future depth requests, 100 levels per book. Record actual starts/ends and schedule lateness. No performance-driven retry or replacement of snapshots. One HTTP attempt per request with a 15-second timeout; rate-limit/denial responses are retained and not circumvented.
- A coherent noncrossed positive two-sided book and exact contract identity/quantity units are required. Reject a pair with request/receipt span over five seconds or available exchange event time over five seconds old relative to the corresponding calibrated server clock. Server clocks must be valid UTC millisecond values; calibration round-trip must be at most five seconds and its half-round-trip uncertainty is retained. Reject events more than one second beyond the calibrated upper clock bound; age admission uses the conservative upper age bound including calibration uncertainty. Missing spot event timestamps remain explicitly freshness-unverified. An API response does not establish simultaneous fills. Clock uncertainty, lack of actual account fee/eligibility evidence and future margin survival prevent executable admission even if conditional economics are positive.

## Conditional cashflow model

Use Decimal arithmetic, real lot/minimum filters and cumulative asks for spot purchase / bids for the future short. Each asset receives the entire stated budget independently; BTC and ETH are alternatives, not a portfolio claiming twice the same capital. Total cash committed includes spot principal, cash entry fees and a total futures reserve of either 1.0 (headline) or exactly 1/3 of futures entry notional. The latter is a capital-efficiency sensitivity, not a conservative stress. No fictitious proceeds from selling a futures contract are credited at entry, and reserve principal is not an expense.

Choose the largest supported futures lot count that fits the stated capital and both displayed books, subject to spot/futures order limits. Quantity selection depends on capacity and budget, never on projected return. Report depth-limited sizing explicitly. Binance spot purchase commission is modeled in received base units: gross up the buy by its fee and round upward to the spot lot step. Preserve and mark the resulting small nonnegative residual spot quantity; do not silently call it perfectly neutral. Spot exit fees are quote-denominated. Third-token discounts, fee rounding, special/tax commissions and actual account rates are not inferred. They require account-specific admission before a strategy run.

Fee scenarios are fixed at spot 0.001 per purchase/sale, futures entry 0.0005 and expiry 0.0005 of their stated notionals, plus a second case doubling all rates. These are labeled scenario assumptions: the public spot table supplies a standard-rate reference, while the futures FAQ explicitly labels its examples hypothetical and the dynamic futures table did not expose rates to the browser. Historical FAQ language equating expiry and taker fees is not sufficient to override current legal terms. Exact applicable commissions and settlement fee basis remain unresolved until authenticated applicability evidence is obtained; no favorable rate is substituted later.

Let g be gross spot units, q be future base units, h=g*(1-spot_entry_fee), A the spot ask-depth cost, B the futures bid-depth notional, E the cash future-entry fee, X the terminal settlement index, and S the executable terminal spot-sale price. In quote units:

`net_cash_profit = h*S + B - q*X - A - E - q*X*expiry_fee - h*S*spot_exit_fee`.

This includes spot-base commission through h, both explicit principals and the unhedged residual h-q. No funding is assumed for the admitted conventional dated future. Reserve principal is released in terminal bookkeeping; this assumes the position survives and is not a margin simulation.

Report the full fixed Cartesian scenarios: terminal index X = initial top spot ask times {0.5,1,2}; adverse spot exit S = X*(1-b/10000) for b in {0,10,50}; both reserve and fee cases; both capital amounts; all three snapshots and both assets. Do not average away adverse cases. Preserve named cash components and terminal NAV, modeled net return on the full capital budget, arithmetic annualization with exact UTC seconds and a 365-day year, plus separately labeled annual cash benchmarks {0,0.03,0.05} accrued simply over the same period. No repeatability or compounding of quoted annualized rates is assumed. No paid financing or credited interest is assumed for the explicitly self-funded, zero-interest-cash scenario; unknown fiat/transfer costs remain excluded qualifications rather than hidden zeros in an executable claim.

For each terminal stress report isolated futures reserve minus marked short loss and expiry charge; a negative value identifies insufficient terminal reserve without external liquidity. Positive reserve does not prove pathwise margin survival, maintenance margin compliance or protection against liquidation. The accounting example includes down/flat/up spot movements and an independent hand-derived reconciliation, including residual exposure, fees, capital and benchmark separation. Log returns never enter cash accounting; a synthetic simple-versus-log contrast exposes the inadmissible convention.

## Decision and continuation

All cells are conditional measurement identities, including unavailable outcomes. No Sharpe, p-value, champion ranking, simulated trading history or financial-ledger append is produced. A necessary screen for considering the next design is positive net cash in all three observations under full reserve, doubled fees, 10bp adverse terminal mismatch and all three terminal-index levels; also report the 3%/5% benchmark comparisons without turning illustrative rates into the user's investment hurdle. This screen is not sufficient for adoption and may reject a setup without establishing permanent absence of opportunities.

Before step 3 can begin, exact fee basis/rates, settlement applicability, account/product availability, reserve/maintenance behavior, source completeness and the user's economic/risk hurdle must be admitted. If these remain unresolved, finish the feasibility report and synthetic example, identify the specific missing evidence, and do not invent a strategy registration or start paper scoring. Market snapshots cannot answer historical robustness. No old settlement recovery, paid purchase, provider contact, live order, VPS edit or scheduler is part of this measurement.
