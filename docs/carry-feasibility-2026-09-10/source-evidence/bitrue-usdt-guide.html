Introduction to Leveraged Trading

Leveraged trading allows users to participate in futures trading without a large amount of capital. Users begin by depositing an amount based on the futures value and leverage the size of their trade by borrowing funds against the deposited amount. Ideally, leveraged trades should be reassessed periodically to ensure that the deposit amount is sufficient to hold the position. If the deposit amount provided by the user is below the sufficient balance needed for the leveraged trade, there is a chance of liquidation. 

 

Features of Perpetual Futures

Most of Bitrue's futures contracts are perpetual futures with no specified expiration date. Users can decide how long they want to hold the futures, which in turn allows them to increase the liquidity of their trade. The price of perpetual futures is based on the asset index, which is equivalent to the average price of the specific asset's spot price and trading volume.

 

Funding Costs

Funding costs represent the net profit/loss amount based on the price difference between the futures market and the spot market. Bitrue updates the funding costs every 8 hours and does not charge this fee. Funding costs are extracted from the accounts with negative balances and paid to accounts with positive balances. More details can be found here.

 

Key Terms

 

Here are some terms you may need to know:

  • Initial Margin, Maintenance Margin, Variation Margin, Available Margin, and Risk Margin.
  • Isolated Margin Mode and Cross Margin Mode.
